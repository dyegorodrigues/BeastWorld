# Current origin and world-graft — WORKING-CANON

> This file is the concise GitHub production-bible expression of the current structural route. For semantic intent and anti-drift nuance, read `docs/00_governance/AUTHORIAL_FIDELITY_PACKET_2026-09-05.md`. For raw nuance, consult the 05/09 author source and semantic record. For detailed human reading, use the current Notion Second Brain encyclopedia.

## 1. Origin
Before present BeastWorld, an older Beast-inhabited Living World existed. Its biodiversity already embodied Quintessence as part of life rather than as a human-discovered external spell system.

This older world was not limited to a few heraldic species. It contained broad independent biodiversity: common, rare, mythical, oceanic, aerial, predatory, plant-like and other forms that may exceed ordinary animal categories.

## 2. Near-extinction
A predatory interworld civilization reached the older Beast World and nearly assimilated/destroyed it. Its strategic strength came from accumulated incorporation: previous conquests could contribute species, techniques, knowledge, servants, biological capabilities and other adaptive resources.

The Beast World did not lose because Beasts were inherently inferior or unintelligent. The current model treats the defeat as a civilizational/adaptive mismatch involving coordination, transfer of knowledge, logistics and accumulated mixed capabilities.

## 3. Survival and sacrifice
The older world failed to preserve its territory but preserved continuity. Deep world-integrated intelligences/entities and other life participated in a survival event that cost original bodies and much of the old biosphere.

Some real remnant escaped carrying enough living continuity to seed/graft a future ecology: matter/substrates, Quintessence, preserved patterns, ecological memory and surviving intelligences/functions.

### Unresolved
The remnant’s exact mass, carrier form, travel physics, duration and in-world name are not defined. `World-seed`, `refugee kernel`, `ark-matrix` and similar phrases are backstage hypotheses, not canon terms.

## 4. Human host world
The remnant reached a younger human-inhabited world that was not yet consciously magical in the BeastWorld sense.

Humans already possessed civilization, warfare, agriculture, medicine, tools, martial traditions, social learning, abstract language and institutions. They therefore contributed something the old Beast ecology had not organized in the same way: cumulative culture and transferable knowledge across generations and contexts.

## 5. Graft and the birth of present BeastWorld
The remnant grafted into the human world rather than replacing it. Present BeastWorld is therefore a **third reality**, produced by the interaction of both histories.

It is not:
- the old Beast World resurrected unchanged;
- a human planet with a detachable magic system installed;
- proof that humans were superior to Beasts.

It is a contested composite whose biology, cultures and institutions have continued to change for approximately three millennia.

The graft is a candidate foundation for a `Year Zero` calendar, but the calendar itself remains unresolved.

## 6. Long co-development
Across millennia:
- host flora and fauna change under Quintessence;
- preserved Beast patterns and current life recombine;
- humans learn to formalize nonhuman patterns into techniques, notation, medicine, alchemy and institutions;
- Sangue becomes a living medium/interface for bestial patterns and recipient biology;
- Matriz becomes part of the stable human–Beast–Quintessence interface;
- Generated Beasts can develop toward autonomous organic personhood;
- founder compacts become Dynastic sovereignties/traditions;
- inter-Dynastic needs eventually justify an Academy integrating martial defense, research, medicine, intelligence, alchemy, artifact work and Fissure study.

None of those mechanisms are assumed to have appeared fully formed at Year Zero.

## 7. Deep entities
The surviving deep entities are more than giant animal gods and less than omnipotent external deities. Their original bodies are gone; their present continuity is integrated with the world.

They may create real bounded material aspects capable of relationship, teaching, negotiation, observation and limited intervention. The aspect is real but is not the total being.

### Unresolved
- exact number;
- exact domains;
- whether four is historical contingency or deeper structure;
- degree to which they created species versus regulated/preserved biodiversity;
- how material aspects are formed and limited;
- what ecological functions each currently sustains.

## 8. Fissures and return of the predator
Fissures are interworld interfaces, not merely monster spawn points. They can bring danger, materials, organisms, peoples, ruins, diseases, knowledge and new laws/conditions.

The old predator eventually finds or re-reaches the composite refuge. Its return is a strategic process independent of the protagonist’s birth or Concession.

Fissures may escalate from isolated anomalies to coordinated probes, infiltration and campaigns. Exact chronology remains open.

## 9. Current thematic opposition
The current structural lens is:

**Can a civilization that grows through reciprocal relationship, accumulated knowledge and negotiated integration out-adapt a civilization that grows by forced incorporation without becoming a copy of its enemy?**

This is not a rule that every inhabitant of BeastWorld is morally good or every inhabitant of the predatory civilization is morally identical. Both societies must contain real conflict, exploitation, dissent and choice.

## 10. Current protagonist route
The main story begins approximately around age fifteen.

The protagonist is already unusually talented and trained. He is not a helpless beginner. He also possesses a real instability/limit that talent alone has not solved.

During the first coordinated Fissure campaign, he receives the first Direct Concession since the Founders. The Concession reorganizes possibility; it does not grant instant mastery.

The adult Director remains a long-term destination earned through training, defeat, campaigns, relationships, leadership, teaching and institutional change.

The cousin remains the legitimate designated successor, independently powerful and not an automatic jealous antagonist.
