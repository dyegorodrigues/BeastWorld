## Current WORKING-CANON spine

1. An older Beast-inhabited Living World existed with broad biodiversity already integrated with Quintessence.
2. A predatory interworld civilization nearly destroyed/assimilated it.
3. Real living continuity escaped as a survival remnant.
4. The remnant reached an initially non-magical human world that already possessed civilization, tools, martial traditions and institutions.
5. The remnant grafted into that world; present BeastWorld is a **new composite born from both histories**.
6. Human cumulative culture, Beast biology, Quintessence and environment co-develop across millennia.
7. Blood, Matrix, Generated Beasts, martial magic, medicine, alchemy, Dynasties and Academy emerge as historical consequences rather than arriving fully formed at Year Zero.
8. The civilization connected to the original near-extinction eventually finds/reaches the refuge again independently of the protagonist.
9. The protagonist’s main lived arc begins around age fifteen; the adult Director is a future state earned through the saga.
10. During the first coordinated Fissure campaign he receives the first Direct Concession since the Founders. It does not grant instant mastery and does not cause the enemy’s return.
11. The cousin remains the legitimate designated successor, independently formidable and not an automatic jealous rival.
12. Reciprocal/negotiated development versus forced/extractive incorporation is the current structural thematic opposition without reducing entire peoples to moral allegories.

## Semantic intent that must remain visible

Do not let the short spine erase these meanings:

- the original Beast World is a whole lost ecology, not four mascot species;
- `Beast` can include oceanic, aerial, terrestrial, predatory, plant-like and stranger forms;
- deep entities must retain numinous/person-like agency; `world-function/archive` is backstage language, not the whole image;
- their original bodies died; present aspects can be physically real yet partial/world-anchored;
- the host humans already had civilization and martial/breathing traditions before accessible magic;
- human culture and Beast/Quintessence biology contribute different strengths to co-development;
- Sangue, Matriz and Bestas Geradas are bodily/developmental/relational, not loot or purity meters;
- Generated Beasts can mature toward autonomous organic personhood;
- earlier CE work preserved an important mechanics distinction when that cluster is reopened: `spectral ≠ immature`, `humanoid ≠ more evolved`, `material density ≠ developmental age`;
- the protagonist begins talented/dangerous but incomplete, can lose, be hurt, need help and keep learning;
- his purpose includes protecting the Living World and its lives, while he may genuinely hunger for power, challenge and worthy opposition;
- mother remains present; possible Oceanic lineage, powerful father/uncle generation and political-marriage-to-love history remain live possibilities;
- Academy needs Coliseum/competition, friendship, romance, humor and ordinary life alongside research, medicine, intelligence, alchemy, logistics and defense;
- field/invader research should visibly change medicine, weapons, doctrine, infrastructure and later enemy responses;
- combat remains grounded in weapon/mass/terrain/injury while scaling to extreme supernatural speed and expression;
- Fissures are interworld frontiers, not generic dungeon generators;
- other Worlds must return durable consequences home;
- enemy remains a civilization capable of learning, infiltration, accumulated incorporation and internal variation;
- resonant notation remains a live thread in which human scholarship formalizes nonhuman/world patterns;
- beauty, brutality, humor, romance, friendship, ordinary life, wonder and war all belong in the intended tone.
