# BeastWorld — Creative Scene Gate

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** GOVERNANCE / NON-LORE  
**Purpose:** prevent system-demonstration scenes, competence collapse, stakes inflation and iterative patch contamination.

## 1. Core rule

A scene exists because a character wants something now and meets meaningful resistance. It must not exist primarily to demonstrate a mechanic, justify a note or showcase a powerful character.

Worldbuilding supports the event from backstage. It does not substitute for drama.

## 2. Mandatory order

Before scene prose, answer in plain language:

| Gate | Required answer |
|---|---|
| dramatic purpose | why does this event belong in the story now? |
| viewpoint | whose experience organizes the scene? |
| desire | what does that person want before the scene ends? |
| resistance | who or what actively prevents it? |
| competence floor | what can every participant reasonably handle? |
| change | what is irreversibly different afterward? |
| causal spine | what five or fewer visible actions produce that change? |

If any answer is absent, do not draft the scene.

## 3. Competence-preservation test

- Would this institution have already trained for the obvious failure?
- Can the threatened character reasonably evade, defend, endure or help?
- Was someone made stupid, fragile or passive only so the protagonist can rescue them?
- Is the danger proportionate to established power?
- Do supporting characters retain initiative?

Any “yes” to competence distortion blocks the scene.

## 4. Anti-contrivance test

Reject or rebuild if:

- a prop or threat appears only to trigger a power demonstration;
- the danger changes identity mid-scene;
- minor failure is inflated into death without scale;
- multiple unrelated elements are packed into one proof;
- the solution requires undefined verbs such as “stabilize,” “reorganize,” “restore,” “contain” or “align”;
- dialogue explains the thesis instead of exposing character;
- politics or morality are asserted without a concrete consequence.

## 5. Scene load limit

A proof scene may carry one primary dramatic burden and at most one secondary revelation.

Examples:

- primary: the Director must make a costly command decision;
- secondary: the audience witnesses a restrained degree of Dominance.

It must not simultaneously prove Academy training, Generated-Beast biology, Phoenix diplomacy, romance, House politics, Dominance, safety law and cosmology.

## 6. Failure protocol

### First structural failure

- stop prose;
- identify whether the idea, mechanism, staging or language failed;
- produce genuinely different alternatives only if the dramatic purpose remains valid.

### Second structural failure

- quarantine the entire scene vehicle;
- do not patch it again in the same context;
- preserve only the underlying discoveries;
- return to story architecture or a different event.

### Author rejection

- mark rejected immediately;
- never label “repaired,” “validated” or “leader” without explicit author acceptance;
- route the rejected construction to COLD history.

## 7. Five-beat physical test

Before storyboard, the event must be expressible as no more than five causal, visible beats:

1. person acts;
2. resistance answers;
3. choice changes the situation;
4. consequence lands;
5. new state remains.

If explanatory paragraphs are required to understand a beat, the mechanics are not ready.

## 8. Prose quality gates

After causal approval, test separately:

1. natural dialogue;
2. viewpoint and emotional continuity;
3. action readability;
4. rhythm and sensory selectivity;
5. visual/transmedia translation;
6. removal of exposition disguised as speech.

A mechanics note is never treated as sample novel prose.

## 9. Context hygiene

HOT context contains:

- current story objective;
- approved character competencies;
- active dramatic question;
- current authorial constraints;
- one directly relevant worldbuilding cluster.

Rejected scene attempts, raw brainstorming and superseded mechanisms remain COLD and are loaded only for avoiding repetition or tracing provenance.

## 10. BeastWorld invariants reinforced

- Academy students are selected talents, not disposable beginners.
- Technique is learned and embodied; Blood/Matrix does not install mastery.
- Action is smart action: terrain, objective, preparation, fear, memory, relationship and consequence.
- Powerful characters do not require everyone else to become incompetent.
- Every scene must alter story state without becoming mechanical formula.
