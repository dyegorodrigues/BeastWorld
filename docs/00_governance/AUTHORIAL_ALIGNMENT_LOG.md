# BeastWorld — Authorial Alignment Log

**Purpose:** living operational memory for interpreting author feedback without flattening voice, reopening settled forks or deleting good ideas because one execution failed.  
**Status:** GOVERNANCE / NON-LORE  
**Load:** WARM for creative synthesis; HOT when resolving ambiguous author feedback.

## Stable communication profile

- Portuguese-BR, natural and direct.
- Begin substantive creative answers with a compact executive overview/table.
- Explain mechanisms through visible situations before technical vocabulary.
- Preserve richness without excessive vertical scrolling.
- Dialogue must sound like adult people speaking, not translated aphorisms.
- The author thinks aloud, revises mid-sentence and may say “esquece / não esquece”; extract the underlying intent rather than treating every fragment as a final decision.
- Praise or criticism may target one layer only. Determine which layer before changing project state.

## Mandatory feedback classification

Before rejecting or promoting anything, classify the feedback separately:

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| idea | does the author like the creative core? |
| mechanism | does the world rule make sense? |
| staging | can the event be visualized causally? |
| language | is the explanation/dialogue natural and clear? |
| canon | was explicit promotion authorized? |

A failure in language does not automatically reject the idea. A good idea does not automatically approve its current staging.

## Known anti-patterns

- abstract labels standing in for explanation;
- ambiguous pronouns and unnamed actors;
- opening old forks after they were settled;
- adding “safe” complications that the author did not request;
- treating a brainstorm as canon;
- deleting a candidate because its first explanation failed;
- dialogue built to state a thesis;
- claiming perfect synchronization without evidence.
