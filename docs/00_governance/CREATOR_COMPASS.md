# BeastWorld — Creator Compass

**Purpose:** one-page cognitive reset for the author when the project feels like a “balaio de gato”.  
**This page is not a canon source.** It is a map of what exists, what is strong, what is still missing, and what to think about next.

## 1. The project in one sentence

A heroic-age fantasy world where human civilizations grew around relationships with extraordinary Great Beasts, later converted access into dynastic monopoly and mythology; the story tests what happens when political ownership, ecological damage, inherited privilege and a deeper metaphysical threat collide.

## 2. The strongest spine we already have

### Political spine

> **A Dinastia não possui a Besta. A Dinastia possui a porta até a Besta.**

The dynastic lie is powerful because it can look statistically true:

`access + training + Echo + time → more opportunities for Recognition`

is misread as:

`chosen blood → rightful ownership / divine legitimacy`.

This is worth protecting.

### Relational/metaphysical spine

Two separate questions:

1. **What does a person want?** — ethical purpose.
2. **How do they relate to another existence?** — reciprocity/consent versus coercion/extraction/appropriation.

That separation prevents “anger = evil / calm = good”.

### Thematic spine

Three recurring tensions:

- inheritance × discipline;
- possession × care;
- death × continuity.

These can appear in family, politics, ecology, magic, Beast relationships and cosmology.

## 3. Things that are vivid enough to keep developing

- Great Species / Great Beasts;
- Ancient = age, not RPG tier;
- Sanctuaries and Guardians;
- Recognition → Bond → Blessing → possible Echo;
- wild populations and Wild Blessing;
- Founding Crime and dynastic propaganda;
- Light as continuity of the individual;
- reconstitution versus reincarnation;
- intention as a real but non-omnipotent variable in Resonance;
- magic deriving from Resonance rather than a second mana system;
- alchemy as legitimate institution, with dangerous branches;
- physical war remaining central;
- combat language: weapons + grappling + shield + fatigue + injuries;
- practical-first live action;
- protagonist pillar / Dragon-dynasty visual development remain laboratories, not locked character canon.

## 4. What is **not** actually decided yet

Do not feel pressure to “remember the answer” because there is no answer yet for many of these:

- final geography;
- names/borders/cities;
- final Dynasties and Houses;
- religions;
- complete peoples/cultures;
- exact beast species list;
- exact metaphysical terminology (`Luz`, `Trevas`, `Inferno`, `Hollow` are placeholders in varying degrees);
- exact magic scarcity/cost envelope;
- exact origin/topology of the corruptive threat;
- exact mechanics of breaches;
- exact longevity/fertility curves;
- exact return/reconstitution costs;
- final character roster;
- final saga/book/season structure;
- final languages;
- far-future other-world expansion.

This incompleteness is **normal project state**, not failure.

## 5. Why the project started to feel impossible

The project has been exploring the highest-abstraction layers — metaphysics and biology — faster than the layers a human imagination uses to “stand somewhere”: geography, named places, concrete people, ordinary life and immediate conflicts.

That creates cognitive vertigo. The cure is not to throw away the metaphysics. It is to give it **ground**.

## 6. The next creative phase should be WORLD SKELETON, not more ontology-only branching

Build a deliberately provisional map that is good enough to force decisions, not pretty enough to become canon by inertia.

Minimum useful skeleton:

1. one rough continental/archipelagic shape;
2. 4–6 major ecological regions;
3. 3–5 provisional political centers/Dynasties;
4. 3–5 Sanctuary systems tied to real habitats, not house mascots;
5. major rivers/coasts/mountain barriers/corridors;
6. 3–5 strategic resources/trade dependencies;
7. 2–3 contested borders/routes;
8. 1–2 weak/interface zones for the threat;
9. 5–7 placeholder characters located on that map;
10. one present-day political crisis and one slow-burn metaphysical/ecological signal.

Names can remain placeholders (`NORTH-COAST-A`, `DYNASTY-C`, `CITY-01`) until the causal geometry works.

## 7. How to reconnect emotionally with the project

Do not try to imagine the whole universe at once.

For each creative session, answer only one concrete camera question:

- Where are we?
- Who is physically in the room?
- What does each person want right now?
- What can they lose before sunset?
- What part of the world does the scene reveal without exposition?

Examples:

- a funeral where everyone knows the dead may theoretically return but the succession cannot wait;
- a Guardian showing a centuries-old recovery ledger whose curves are getting worse;
- a dynastic feast where a wild Beast recognizes the wrong person;
- a border ambush that demonstrates the local martial doctrine;
- an alchemist treating a Beast injury while a court politician asks for something ethically dubious;
- a Sanctuary whose migration corridor has been blocked by a profitable city.

One scene can reveal politics + ecology + character + metaphysics simultaneously.

## 8. Working rule for external AI ideas

Claude/Gemini/Grok/ChatGPT proposals remain in external-review quarantine until:

`research → comparison → author understands → author decides → integration → lore regression → GitHub/Notion sync`.

A good proposal is not a decision.

## 9. Current maintenance priorities before any promotion

1. keep the new metaphysics/threat layer represented in the graph;
2. reconcile Notion-only status drift for Dragons;
3. preserve full option wording for letter-coded author choices;
4. repair human navigation (including 10B);
5. keep Source Archive security/provenance explicit;
6. do not merge bootstrap to main without explicit author authorization.

## 10. The author does not need to solve this alone

The operating model is intentionally:

- author supplies taste, intuition, acceptance/rejection and emotional truth;
- agents perform retrieval, consistency checking, research, alternative generation and dependency analysis;
- no agent may silently convert its proposal into the author's intention;
- no single session needs to hold the whole universe in working memory.

When overwhelmed, return to this page and then to the single next concrete layer being built.
