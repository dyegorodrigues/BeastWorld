# BeastWorld — Creative Horizon Map

**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** META / DESIGN ROUTER / NON-CANON  
**Purpose:** anticipate future problems and opportunities without contaminating the current gate with premature solutions.

## Operating rule

This map is not a backlog that forces the story to include every idea. It stores candidate consequences, proof scenes, dependencies and research routes. Items become active only when their prerequisites mature. No entry promotes lore.

## Current north star

Build one adult epic fantasy property that combines:

- civilizational depth, dynasties, memory, politics and myth;
- biologically coherent but overtly magical bestial evolution;
- fast, lethal, readable martial combat;
- iconic weapons, silhouettes, transformations and Fields;
- emotionally adult characters, sensuality, consequence and agency;
- one story-mother prepared for novel, webtoon/manhwa-style visual adaptation, anime and live action;
- density without filler, game-like progression or exposition dumps.

## H2 — Narrative proof scenes

### Phoenix Elder and Director

- Phoenix is a Generated Beast from an old and important generation of the lineage—not a primordial natural Beast or a millennia-old creature;
- `Elder` marks house standing, maturity and power; she may be the Phoenix House's strongest elder or strongest living member—candidate only;
- her developmental origin may have been a formidable matriarch/Sovereign—open;
- Director provisionally 35–40, earlier seed 35;
- chemistry: two powerful adults, age asymmetry without maturity/consent asymmetry;
- the proposed attraction-test transformation scene is discarded; any future Full Form appearance must earn its own dramatic cause;
- proof target: sensuality + hierarchy + trust/danger without using transformation as exposition.

### Director enters an army

- arrive via Dragon/Bestial partner, not universal personal flight;
- missile-like drop, readable impact, continuous lateral sword passage;
- retain ground geography and martial contact;
- fight must change strategic and emotional state, not exist only as spectacle.

## H3 — Combat grammar

- body amplification → weapon expression → contact emission → environmental control → Field/Domain-like effects with anchors and consequences;
- preserve deflection, footwork, timing, sword contact, grappling/kicks and bodily risk;
- reserve large magic for causal escalation rather than replacing martial competence;
- produce three combat proofs: duel, small-team hunt, mass battlefield.

## H4 — Story-mother and adaptation views

Create a canonical scene record with:

- scene purpose and state change;
- POV/desire/conflict;
- world/lore revealed through action;
- canonical visual event and combat geography;
- novel expression;
- webtoon vertical/panel expression;
- anime timing/sound/choreography expression;
- live-action physical/VFX expression;
- continuity dependencies and production risks.

The schema supports adaptation; it must not turn prose drafting into a form-filling exercise.

## Horizon guardrails

- no hypothesis becomes canon because it appears here;
- no reference becomes a checklist or plagiarism source;
- future-world ideas do not interrupt the current world's emotional establishment;
- each medium contributes its strengths rather than receiving a mechanical copy;
- “every scene changes something” is applied in revision, not as robotic prose generation;
- the author receives only meaningful forks; backstage complexity remains routed here.

## H6 — Authorial signature and finite route — Pass 46

Creative evaluation must preserve:

- spectacle with consequence;
- progression that remains bodily, martial and intelligible;
- magic enlarging rather than erasing physical competence;
- deep foundations without endless exposition;
- powerful characters with presence, desire, contradiction and agency;
- rewarding chapters without cheap cliffhanger dependence;
- active critique, research and proposal from the assistant rather than passive validation.

Planned route: close CE-05 → consolidate Core Engine v0.9 → character proofs → three combat proofs → civilizational propagation → Director/Book 1 architecture. Round numbers are estimates and disappear when no real fork remains.
