# ADR-0006 — Promote the origin-graft and young-protagonist route to WORKING-CANON

## Context
Pass 55 reconstructed the author’s 05/09 origin brainstorm and stress-tested one causal architecture capable of joining Beasts, humans, Quintessence, Blood, Matrix, Generated Beasts, Dynasties, Academy, Fissures, war, exploration, combat progression and the protagonist’s long arc.

The author subsequently reviewed the reconstruction and explicitly validated the direction with the statement that this is “the path we are going to follow”, while also asking for a detailed second-brain rebuild and warning that fine details may still be corrected after close reading.

Under `CANON_POLICY.md`, that combination supports `WORKING-CANON`, not final `CANON`.

## Decision
Adopt the following as the current authoritative working route:

1. **Older Beast World.** Before present BeastWorld, an older Living World existed whose biodiversity already embodied Quintessence.
2. **Near-extinction.** A predatory interworld civilization nearly destroyed/assimilated that world.
3. **Survival remnant.** Some real living continuity escaped: ecological patterns, Quintessence, matter/substrate, memory and surviving deep/world-integrated intelligences. Exact carrier/mechanism remains unresolved.
4. **Human host world.** The remnant reached an initially non-magical human world possessing mature human culture, tools, martial traditions and institutions but lacking the Beast-world form of conscious Quintessence integration.
5. **Graft.** The remnant grafted into that world. Present BeastWorld is the **new composite produced by that merger**, not the old world restored and not the human world with a magic overlay.
6. **Reciprocal development.** Human cumulative culture and Beast/Quintessence biology co-develop over millennia, producing the historical conditions from which Blood, Matrix, Generated Beasts, martial-magic systems, alchemy, medicine, founding compacts, Dynasties and eventually the Academy can emerge.
7. **Return of the predator.** The civilization connected to the original near-extinction eventually rediscovers/reaches the refuge through the interworld/Fissure problem. The protagonist does not summon this return merely by existing.
8. **Young chronology.** The protagonist’s main lived arc begins approximately at age fifteen. He is already highly trained and talented but genuinely incomplete. The adult Director remains a destination earned later.
9. **Book 1 axis.** During the first coordinated Fissure campaign, the protagonist receives the first Direct Concession since the Founders and must gain agency over its consequences while evidence of the old predator’s identity emerges.
10. **Legitimate cousin.** The cousin remains the designated legitimate successor, independently formidable and not an automatic jealous rival or humiliation ladder.
11. **Thematic opposition.** The current structural question contrasts reciprocal development/integration with forced or extractive incorporation. This is a dramatic lens, not a rule that every member of one civilization is morally identical.

## Why this route was chosen
### Causal compression
It converts previously parallel ideas into consequences of one historical event.

### Character compatibility
It preserves the desired adult Director without sacrificing visible growth, defeat, family formation, Academy life, friendship, romance, teaching and command.

### Civilizational scale
It gives the Academy, medicine, alchemy, martial doctrine and Dynasties reasons to exist beyond genre convention.

### Ecological scale
It permits current BeastWorld to remain contested and plural rather than a perfectly harmonious super-organism.

### Antagonist scale
It produces an enemy whose strategic method can adapt and learn without requiring a flat ‘dark = evil’ cosmology.

### Reversibility
The decision fixes the **spine**, not the exact physiology, terminology, map, calendar, entity count or scene sequence.

## Stress-test receipt
The route has been examined against the required scales sufficiently for WORKING-CANON:

- **individual:** protagonist limitation, Direct Concession, training, injury, agency and cousin relationship;
- **community:** family, mixed lineages, Beast personhood, Academy competition/research, access to Blood/Matrix;
- **state/civilization:** Dynasties, Academy coordination, military doctrine, research, logistics and enemy infiltration;
- **ecological/world:** graft stability, Beast biodiversity, world-integrated entities, Fissures and interworld consequences;
- **Devil’s Advocate:** Pass 55 explicitly tested literal planet fragments, four creators of all life, evolutionary destiny, unlimited entity avatars, instant-mastery Concession, purity percentages, protagonist-caused invasion and infinite consume-world escalation.

## Explicit non-decisions
The ADR does **not** decide:

- the exact form, mass, name or travel mechanics of the survival remnant;
- whether exactly four deep entities exist or survived;
- whether those entities created species or regulated/preserved an independent biosphere;
- exact Blood synthesis, reservoirs, inheritance or Direct Concession mechanism;
- exact age/date of the first Fissures;
- exact maternal/Oceanic genealogy;
- exact Dynastic count, titles, borders and governance;
- exact manifestation of the Direct Concession;
- exact ethics/mechanics of future world integration or salvage;
- concrete romance configuration;
- Book 1 opening scene, enemy champion, climax or ending.

These remain `HYPOTHESIS` or `UNRESOLVED` and must be labeled as such.

## Consequences
### Positive
- Current creative work may build consistently on this spine.
- Notion can be reorganized around a human-readable current encyclopedia instead of V3/V4 entry pages.
- GitHub HOT pointers no longer need to say ‘waiting for author validation of the four gates’.
- Future research can target exact open mechanisms instead of repeatedly reopening the whole premise.

### Required safeguards
- Any future change to the spine requires a new ADR or explicit supersession note.
- Old pages are archived/superseded, not silently deleted.
- The raw 05/09 author brainstorm remains immutable provenance.
- No detail becomes CANON simply because it appears in the new Wiki.
