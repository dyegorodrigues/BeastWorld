# Round 1 v2 — Initial stress tests and layered hierarchy hypothesis

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Status:** `RESEARCH / HYPOTHESIS / NOT CANON`

## Test 1 — two fighters from the same lineage
Question: can two elite fighters with similar inherited bestial history feel radically different without one secretly belonging to another magic system?

### Engine I — Metabolismo Bestial
Works well for shared ancestry and bodily plausibility, but by itself tends to make same-lineage fighters converge around similar physiological advantages.

It can generate differences through conditioning, injury, adaptation and specialization, but risks making individuality look like a sub-build of the same biological class.

### Engine II — Concordância Viva
Can differentiate fighters through different weapons, Bestas, environments, relational history and resonant preferences. Strong for relational identity, but if made universal it can make every difference depend on invisible `compatibility` language.

### Engine III — Gramática Marcial Viva
Strongest differentiation in this test. Same biological inheritance can produce completely different fighting identities because technique is authored through training history, chosen operations, sequencing, risk tolerance, weapon and tactical imagination.

**Finding:** shared biology + personal grammar produces stronger same-lineage individuality than biology alone.

## Test 2 — human + Besta team vs an intelligent enemy that knows their school
Question: does the system generate counterplay and adaptation rather than larger attacks?

### Engine I
Enemy can exploit activation patterns, metabolism, injury and known biological limitations. Good physical counterplay. Besta agency needs additional relational rules or risks becoming a biological accessory.

### Engine II
Strongest at making the Besta a real tactical partner. Enemy can disrupt synchronization, force incompatible anchors, isolate one partner or exploit disagreement. Excellent team-story yield, provided `resonance` is tightly constrained.

### Engine III
Enemy can read known school sequences and force improvisation. The fighter must recompose techniques rather than unlock a bigger output. Excellent competence/action storytelling.

**Finding:** personal grammar creates adaptive martial chess; concordance preserves Besta personhood and team dynamics; metabolism keeps consequences physical.

## Current favorite hierarchy — NOT a decision
A promising architecture is emerging:

### Layer 1 — biological ontology
**Metabolismo Bestial** explains why a BeastWorld body can interact with Quintessence at all.

Blood and Matrix belong primarily here.

### Layer 2 — learned martial expression
**Gramática Marcial Viva** explains what training does and why same-lineage fighters become radically different people in combat.

Schools, breathing, body mechanics, weapons, technique and visible progression belong primarily here.

### Layer 3 — relational/metabiological interface
**Concordância Viva** explains relationships that truly involve another living intelligence or larger ecology: Besta, Besta Gerada, Entity, perhaps some environmental/world interactions and Direct Concession.

It should not be used as a magic word for ordinary technique when biology/skill already explain the event.

## Why this is hierarchy rather than fusion
Each layer receives a distinct question:

- **biology:** what can this organism sustain?
- **martial grammar:** what has this person learned to do with it?
- **concordance:** with whom/what can this person enter a meaningful living relationship?

A mechanism that cannot identify which question it answers is probably redundant.

## Direct Concession under this hierarchy
Candidate model:

1. the Entity establishes/permits a new concordant relationship;
2. that relationship changes what the recipient's Matrix/body can potentially sustain;
3. the recipient still lacks a mature martial grammar for those new possibilities;
4. later training authors techniques from that new capacity.

This preserves all four desired effects:
- Concession is personal and relational;
- it can create a real biological/metabiological change;
- it opens unprecedented possibility;
- it does not install mastery.

## Entity communication under this hierarchy
Use a bounded bandwidth model:

1. **Presence** — pressure, orientation, emotion, no proposition;
2. **Impression** — image, danger, memory fragment, semantic impulse;
3. **Directed contact** — deliberate short dialogue/meaning exchange;
4. **Embodiment** — material aspect capable of sustained ordinary interaction.

Higher bandwidth requires more attention, compatibility or manifestation cost. The Entity has its own agenda and is neither omniscient nor always available.
