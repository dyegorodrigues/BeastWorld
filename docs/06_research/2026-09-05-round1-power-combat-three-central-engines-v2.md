# Round 1 v2 — Three central Power & Combat engines

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Status:** `RESEARCH / HYPOTHESIS / AUTHOR ROOM / NOT CANON`

## Why v2 exists
The first comparison used `Corpo Vivo`, `Ressonância` and `Gramática Pessoal` as if they were competing systems. They were not clean alternatives. They mostly answered different questions.

This v2 compares three genuinely different answers to:

> **What is the central engine that makes a BeastWorld fighter powerful?**

All three are tested through the same scenario.

## Shared test scenario
A ~15-year-old elite martial trainee with a curved sword protects an injured companion in a narrow ruin/corridor during an early Fissure operation. A heavily armored nonhuman predator charges. Its outer structure disperses obvious heat/Quintessence attacks. Retreat would expose the injured person.

A useful system must tell us:
- what the fighter perceives;
- what he intentionally changes;
- what his body/weapon/Beast actually do;
- why he cannot just output more power;
- how the enemy can counter him;
- what a more experienced fighter would do differently;
- what training can improve afterward.

---

# ENGINE I — Metabolismo Bestial

## Felt image
Power feels **inside the flesh first**. Before light, flame or supernatural spectacle, the body changes what it is capable of sustaining.

A great fighter does not look like a wizard who learned swordsmanship. He looks like a martial organism whose respiration, senses, connective tissue, reflexes, blood chemistry and Quintessence handling have been trained into something human biology could not normally become.

## Central engine
The body is the primary machine that converts Quintessence into capability.

Possible role hierarchy:
- **Quintessence:** universal substrate/energy/field available in the Living World;
- **Blood:** inherited living pattern/catalytic compatibility derived historically from bestial integration;
- **Matrix:** adaptive regulatory interface that allows a specific body to metabolize, stabilize and route bestial/Quintessence patterns;
- **martial training:** teaches the body when, where and how to express capability;
- **weapon:** extends the body's force/Quintessence pathways but does not replace technique;
- **Besta/Besta Gerada:** co-developed life capable of adding perception, morphology, coordination or complementary biological capability without becoming a disposable spell;
- **Entity:** rare deeper biological/metabiological source or relationship, not the everyday engine.

## Same corridor fight
The predator dissipates a direct heat release.

The fighter does not answer with a larger fire attack. He changes his own state: shortens breath rhythm, shifts reinforcement into legs/hips/forearm, increases sensory processing, drives a brief high-torque entry, uses the sword's real edge and physical geometry, then releases Quintessence only at the moment/point where the armor cannot distribute the load.

The magic makes the martial action physically impossible by normal-human standards, but the **fight remains readable as body + weapon + timing**.

## Progression
Visible growth comes from:
- higher efficiency;
- better local control instead of whole-body flooding;
- larger safe operating envelope;
- faster transitions between bodily states;
- recovery;
- new bestial adaptations becoming usable;
- better weapon integration;
- deeper coordination with a Besta.

## Cost/counterplay
- metabolic debt;
- heat/oxygen/resource depletion;
- tissue damage;
- neural overload;
- Blood/Matrix mismatch;
- injury disrupting a specific pathway;
- poison/environment altering metabolism;
- enemy learning where activation creates vulnerability.

## Direct Concession under this engine
Concession could alter the **possible operating envelope** of the body/Matrix or introduce access to a primordial pattern that the fighter still must learn to express.

It does not install techniques.

## Entity communication
Possible, but not inherent to ordinary power use. A rare Matrix/Concession relationship could act as a channel for semantic/resonant contact.

## Strengths
- excellent martial grounding;
- supports medicine, anatomy, alchemy and training;
- visible progression;
- makes injury meaningful;
- preserves Fog Hill-like physical intelligence;
- fits the long biological history of BeastWorld.

## Main risk
Bloodline determinism. If inherited biology explains too much, talent becomes pedigree and martial learning loses dignity.

Secondary risk: backstage biology can become sterile pseudo-science if reader-facing explanation is overexposed.

# ENGINE II — Concordância Viva

## Felt image
Power happens **between living things**, not only inside one body.

A fighter is dangerous because he can bring body, bestial pattern, weapon, environment, Besta and Quintessence into a precise relationship. A stronger fighter is not simply a larger battery. He can hold more complex relationships without losing himself.

## Central engine
Resonant coupling is primary.

Possible role hierarchy:
- **Quintessence:** field/substrate through which living patterns can couple;
- **Blood:** inherited affinity/memory that makes certain relationships easier or safer;
- **Matrix:** living resonant chamber/translator that keeps incompatible patterns from tearing the person apart;
- **Besta/Besta Gerada:** an independent second subject in the relationship, not equipment;
- **martial training:** teaches timing, synchronization, interruption, redirection and controlled dissonance;
- **weapon:** a tuned mediator that can carry or alter resonance;
- **Entity:** an enormous intelligent node in the same Living-World ecology, making semantic contact conceptually natural.

## Same corridor fight
The predator dissipates direct heat.

The fighter stops treating it as a wall to overpower. He reads the creature's pulse/movement pattern, changes the coupling between his footwork, blade and local Quintessence, uses his Besta's perception or another living reference to catch the instant the armor's plates reconfigure, then strikes during a moment of **resonant incoherence**.

A master might deliberately create dissonance in one layer and harmony in another, tricking the enemy into defending the wrong interaction.

## Progression
Growth means:
- perceiving more relationships;
- stabilizing stronger/stranger resonances;
- switching anchors faster;
- coordinating with another person/Besta without losing autonomy;
- weapon tuning;
- maintaining self-coherence under hostile fields;
- eventually negotiating relationships with world-scale intelligences.

## Cost/counterplay
- dissonance/feedback;
- interference;
- environment mismatch;
- enemy severing/poisoning an anchor;
- emotional state affecting precision but **not automatically granting friendship power**;
- relationship/consent problems with sentient Bestas;
- Entity has its own agenda and cannot be treated as a summon button.

## Direct Concession under this engine
Concession is a deliberate **new stable relationship** offered by an Entity. It allows contact with a scale/pattern previously impossible but gives no automatic control.

## Entity communication
This engine supports it most naturally.

Communication can exist at different bandwidths:
1. **presence:** pressure/emotional or directional sense, no words;
2. **impression:** image, warning, memory fragment, semantic packet;
3. **direct contact:** brief language/dialogue when both sides deliberately align;
4. **embodiment:** physical aspect and ordinary conversation at much greater manifestation cost.

The Entity is not an omniscient assistant. It has limited attention, perspective, priorities and perhaps poor understanding of human assumptions.

## Strengths
- structurally embodies BeastWorld's reciprocity theme;
- gives Entity relationships real mechanics;
- lets place/environment matter;
- supports interpersonal combat/teamwork without making everyone identical;
- makes Concession relational by nature.

## Main risk
Vagueness. Without hard writer-room constraints, `resonance` becomes a magic word that explains everything.

Secondary risk: accidentally becoming emotion/power-of-friendship logic.

# ENGINE III — Artes de Expressão / Gramática Marcial Viva

## Felt image
Everyone inhabits the same world and has bodies shaped by its biology, but mastery looks like **authorship**.

A novice can possess monstrous potential and still have a small vocabulary. A master can do something terrifying with modest raw output because he can compose actions no one else sees coming.

## Central engine
Learned operations and personal composition are primary.

Backstage model:
- **Quintessence:** manipulable substrate;
- **Blood:** supplies inherited primitives/affinities, sensory biases and physiological possibilities;
- **Matrix:** keeps operations coherent and embodied, translating intent/training into stable living execution;
- **martial traditions:** teach fundamental operations/`verbs`, combinations, timing and constraints;
- **weapon:** changes what combinations are physically possible;
- **Besta/Besta Gerada:** contributes another perspective/body/repertoire rather than a premade skill list;
- **personal style:** emerges from which operations a person chooses, chains, conditions and embodies;
- **Entity/Concession:** may expose one or more previously inaccessible primitive possibilities, not a finished move.

`Verb` and `grammar` are writer-room lenses. Characters need not talk like programmers.

## Same corridor fight
The predator dissipates heat.

The fighter's solution is not `stronger fire`. His repertoire lets him combine operations in a sequence: anchor his stance, compress force, delay Quintessence release, redirect the armor's own movement, then extend/shear only at the blade's final contact.

Another fighter from the **same lineage** might solve the identical problem through completely different operations: sensory prediction + lateral propulsion + impact absorption + countercut.

Same ancestry. Different martial sentence.

## Progression
Growth becomes visible as:
- new fundamental operations;
- greater precision;
- chaining without pauses;
- simultaneous operations;
- new conditions/uses discovered through experience;
- weapon/body integration;
- ability to improvise under incomplete information;
- personal techniques emerging from a history of choices rather than being inherited move lists.

This is the strongest home for the Pass 59 hypothesis **progression by verbs**.

## Cost/counterplay
- each operation loads body/Matrix differently;
- complex chains create coherence/attention cost;
- disrupting one link can collapse a technique;
- fighters can read and counter known schools;
- unusual sequencing can surprise stronger opponents;
- injury can remove access to part of a person's repertoire;
- bad composition can waste enormous output.

## Direct Concession under this engine
Concession gives the protagonist **new possible grammar**, not prose already written.

He may suddenly possess access to a capability nobody has had since the Founders while still being clumsy, unsafe or creatively immature in its use.

## Entity communication
Not automatically part of the ordinary martial engine. It can be added as a special relational channel, potentially intensified by Concession.

## Strengths
- enormous individual character differentiation;
- techniques can express personality;
- progression can be seen rather than narrated as ranks;
- supports Nano Machine's competence pleasure without nanotech;
- supports JJK/HxH-style individualized problem solving without copying their mechanics;
- supports Fog Hill-like choreography because supernatural actions stay attached to martial decisions.

## Main risk
Overformalization. If the story exposes `verbs`, syntax trees or operation charts literally, BeastWorld can start feeling like a programming RPG.

The grammar should primarily guide writers/choreographers. Reader understanding should emerge through repeated action and simple in-world language.

# Important conclusion — hierarchy may matter more than fusion
The three engines can technically coexist, but **coexistence is not automatically good design**.

A possible hierarchy to test, not adopt yet:

1. **physical ontology:** Engine I explains why the body can do magic at all;
2. **ordinary martial expression:** Engine III explains why training and personal style matter;
3. **special relational layer:** selected Engine II principles explain Besta/Entity/world relationships and Direct Concession.

That is not `A+B+C because all sound cool`. It is assigning each concept one job and blocking duplicate explanations.

Alternative hierarchies remain possible:
- Engine II could be fundamental, with biology/grammar derived from resonance;
- Engine I could dominate almost everything, making resonance rare;
- Engine III could be the reader-facing truth while biology remains mostly backstage.

Round 1 must decide the hierarchy through story tests.

# Reference-engineering notes
- Fog Hill creator interviews emphasize weapon/fighting style chosen according to body/personality and distinguish more realistic weapon combat from increasingly superhuman combat modes. This supports physical readability, not its five-element cosmology.
- Solo Leveling production interviews explicitly describe representing level growth in the changing physical quality of action, and its action director emphasizes very high speed that remains visually perceptible. This supports visible progression/readability, not game UI.
- Fullmetal Alchemist's official introduction demonstrates causal compression: a small number of rules/taboos generates body, family, military, oppression and national-conspiracy consequences. This supports BeastWorld's complexity-budget principle.
- Jujutsu Kaisen remains a reference for individualized techniques, relationships, institutions and apex-character strategic gravity, not a package of cursed-energy mechanics.
