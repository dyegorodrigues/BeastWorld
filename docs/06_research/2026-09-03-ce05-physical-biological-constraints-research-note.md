# Research Note — CE-05 Physical Constraints and Biological Analogies

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** RESEARCH / INSPIRATION BOUNDARY / NOT-CANON  
**Question:** Which real mechanisms help BeastWorld remain causally legible without pretending that Deep Body is established science?

## Findings

1. **Metamorphosis is coordinated remodeling, not random inflation.** Amphibian metamorphosis is regulated across many tissues by interacting endocrine pathways. BeastWorld can borrow the design principle that a body-wide change needs a coordinating signal and synchronized tissue response.
2. **Living systems can retain anatomical targets/pattern information.** Developmental-bioelectricity and planarian work discuss distributed physiological states that influence anatomical patterning and regeneration. This is an analogy for a Quintessential body map, not proof of supernatural forms.
3. **Regeneration depends on position and context.** Salamander limb regeneration research emphasizes positional information; zebrafish research shows that some vertebrates can repair adult neural tissue through coordinated cellular responses. This supports graded, tissue-specific regeneration rather than a generic healing percentage.
4. **Morphology changes locomotion.** Bird and morphing-flight studies show that centre of gravity, moment of inertia, wing geometry and configuration change stability and manoeuvrability. Full Form should therefore change what motion is easy, not simply add “more power.”
5. **Mass plus velocity creates momentum.** Ordinary mechanics makes free preservation of velocity during abrupt mass coupling a combat exploit. BeastWorld’s fantastic law should require impulse/control to accelerate newly opened anatomy.

## Inspiration boundary

None of these sources demonstrates nonlocal living anatomy, Quintessence, magical human limb regeneration, dual biological morphologies, anti-Quintessence or magical giant flight. They constrain consequences and vocabulary. The actual law is fictional.
