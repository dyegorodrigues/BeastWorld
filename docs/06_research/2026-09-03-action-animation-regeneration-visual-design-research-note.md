# Research Note — Action Animation, Regeneration and Visual Design

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** RESEARCH / ANALOGY / NOT-CANON

## 1. Animation vocabulary

- **Genga** refers to key-animation drawings that establish pivotal motion; **douga** supplies in-between drawings. It is not the name of a fighting style.
- **Sakuga** is broader audience/industry language for noteworthy animation/drawing craft; it is not a martial system.
- Useful production vocabulary for BeastWorld: action/fight choreography; spatial geography; layout/camera choreography; key poses; anticipation; timing and spacing; frame-rate modulation; smear frames; impact frames; hit-stop-like emphasis; follow-through; secondary motion; and effects animation.
- Fast combat remains intelligible when the spectator can read preparation, trajectory, contact, reaction and consequence even if individual transitions are aggressively compressed.

## 2. Selective action references

Reference extraction, not imitation:
- *Sword of the Stranger*: lethal sword contact, environmental reaction, bodily weight and escalating duel clarity.
- *Jujutsu Kaisen*: body/magic causal interplay and combat that reveals rules through tactical use.
- *Naruto*’s Madara battlefield sequence: one-versus-many phrasing with memorable spatial beats.
- *Solo Leveling*: silhouette, speed, impact and effects escalation.
- *Bleach*: weapon identity and spiritual presence.
- *Seirei no Moribito*, *Fog Hill of Five Elements*, *Castlevania* and *Arcane*: future targeted study candidates for polearms, martial motion, multi-actor brutality and character acting within action.

## 3. Regeneration analogies

Biological research supports the design intuition that regeneration needs more than raw energy:
- salamander limb regeneration depends on positional information/memory and tissue signaling;
- regeneration recruits metabolic resources over sustained repair;
- organ systems vary widely in repair/regenerative capacity;
- mammalian central nervous tissue is far more limited than peripheral systems, so BeastWorld brain repair remains explicitly supernatural and continuity-bounded.

Sources:
- Nature (2025), molecular basis of positional memory: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09036-5
- Kübler et al., metabolic needs of regeneration: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7613859/
- Positional Memory in Vertebrate Regeneration: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9248832/
- Neuronal redevelopment and CNS regeneration: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9074815/

These sources provide analogies, not proof that humans can regrow organs, limbs or brain tissue.

## 4. Costume/material-culture method

Arms and armor historically function as protection, art, status and ceremonial display. Fight books also preserve how armor, civilian fighting clothes and weapons function in motion. BeastWorld should therefore design wardrobe from:
1. social office and lineage;
2. real movement/weapon clearance;
3. climate/ecology/materials;
4. ceremonial/status layer;
5. magical/resonant function;
6. repeated silhouette language.

Sources:
- Metropolitan Museum Arms and Armor: https://www.metmuseum.org/departments/arms-and-armor
- Met, Talhoffer fight book and equipment in motion: https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/hans-talhoffers-fight-book-a-sixteenth-century-manuscript-about-the-art-of-fighting

## Research conclusion

The user’s desired “Kenga/Jenga” is closest to a package of **genga/key animation + sakuga-level execution + action choreography + kinetic staging**. BeastWorld needs an original martial grammar underneath it so spectacle remains character-, weapon- and lineage-specific.
