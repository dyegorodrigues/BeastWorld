## Author corrections captured
### Living World
`Mundo Vivo` is not simply a planet containing life. It is a planet whose matter, biosphere and Quintessence have achieved planetary-scale metabiological organization/agency.

### Deep Entities
Do not describe them merely as organs that later acquired personhood. Better current hypothesis: persistent individuated centers of the Living World's own planetary life, simultaneously part of the whole and genuine persons.

### Relational perception
Do not confuse self-owned enhanced senses/instinct with information intentionally shared by Beast/Entity.

### Quintessence
The universal-vs-world-local question is reopened and now the highest-value remaining Round 1 cosmology question.

### Matrix
The author likes a distributed Matrix and severe regeneration, but wants it to remain adaptive rather than a cultivation-core vulnerability. External alchemical geometry and internal living Matrix should be tested as separate layers.

### Martial/elemental design
Elemental expression remains required. Fire, Water, Air and Earth-like surfaces can exist without turning states into Avatar nations or collapsing each Dynasty into one animal/element.

### Protagonist
The Pass62 `cross-lineage seam/coherence bottleneck` is **WITHDRAWN** as preferred implementation. Do not route future work through inherent hybrid dysfunction.

### Static future weakness payoff
The example `enemy studies old seam and attacks same transition years later` is also withdrawn. Enemy adaptation remains, but must respect protagonist improvisation and dynamic counter-adaptation.

## Strong new research candidates — NOT CANON
1. **One Quintessence / three regimes:** free cosmic → world-organized → assimilated/bound.
2. **Living World threshold:** an inhabited world becomes a Living World only after large-scale Quintessential/metabiological integration.
3. **Entity architecture:** focal planetary persons / one-and-many agency.
4. **Matrix two-layer model:** external integration geometry induces an internal distributed living Matrix.
5. **Elemental grammar:** visible elemental surfaces derive from deeper modes such as transformation, flow, propagation and structure.
6. **Derived expressions:** Lightning from high-order Fire/Dragon-family control; Ice from Oceanic/flow-phase control; other derivatives emerge by mechanism/evolution, not skill-tree unlocks.
7. **Protagonist compositional model:** two legitimate repertoires expand expressive vocabulary instead of creating a congenital defect.
