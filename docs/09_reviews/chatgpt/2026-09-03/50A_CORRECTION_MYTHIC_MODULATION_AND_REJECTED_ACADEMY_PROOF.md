## 2. Two different transformations

### Irreversible: Mythic passage

A Natural Beast may accumulate the biological, resonant, ecological and material prerequisites for a Mythic passage. It is not Mythic until the passage succeeds.

The passage is a qualitative reorganization of the same individual:

- Matrix and inheritance;
- Quintessence regulation;
- available organs and morphologies;
- longevity, regeneration and reproduction;
- ecological effect and potential ceiling.

The exact requirements remain open. Readiness, attempt, failure and completed passage must not be collapsed.

### Reversible: adult morphological modulation

After a successful Mythic passage, humanoid morphology becomes an intrinsic available configuration. The Beast can open or recollect between humanoid and bestial forms according to need and preference.

This alternation is not a repeated evolution and does not create a second body. It is one organism using two supported morphologies.

## 4. Generated-Beast maturity must be decomposed

“Young Beast” is unusable without specifying the axis.

A Generated Beast can be:

- newly distinct as an identity but cognitively sophisticated through co-development;
- powerful in field manifestation but inexperienced in autonomous embodiment;
- mature as a person while still dependent on a human developmental niche;
- old chronologically but unskilled in one morphological transition.

We must not assume that it is an infant because its autonomous body is new. We also must not assume centuries of literal personal memories merely because its Matrix is old. Memory inheritance remains an independent gate.
