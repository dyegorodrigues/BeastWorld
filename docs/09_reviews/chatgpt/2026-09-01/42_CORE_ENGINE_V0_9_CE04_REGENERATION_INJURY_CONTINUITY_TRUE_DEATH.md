> Reader-facing question: why can one marked fighter close a sword wound while another needs a surgeon; why can an apex survive a spear through the brain but still truly die?

## 1. Recommended model — regenerative competence, not a healing spell

Regeneration is a property of the transformed living system. Matrix, Mark, nervous/endocrine-like interfaces, Quintessence regulation and the organism’s preserved body-pattern cooperate to resume development at the wound.

Backstage, successful regeneration depends on six requirements:

1. **continuity:** enough of the person and control network remains active;
2. **pattern:** the organism still knows what structure belongs there and where;
3. **competent tissue/interface:** local cells/material can enter a regenerative state;
4. **control:** the body can coordinate growth rather than scar, tumor, malformed tissue or runaway bestial expression;
5. **substrate:** water, salts, proteins, minerals, biomass and lineage-specific material are available;
6. **capacity/time:** Quintessence, metabolic work, circulation, protection and time are sufficient.

External biology supports the usefulness of pattern memory as an analogy: axolotl limb regeneration preserves and can experimentally rewrite positional identity; mammalian digit-tip regeneration also shows that regenerative competence can depend strongly on the level and tissue environment of amputation. These are analogies, not BeastWorld law.

## 2. Regeneration ladder — tissue/scale, not public RPG rank

| Band | Typical capability | Operating mode | Expected cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RG-0 repair` | clotting, skin, bruising, small tears | automatic | low/ordinary |
| `RG-1 accelerated healing` | deep cuts, vessels, muscle, uncomplicated fractures | automatic then recovery | fatigue, heat, hunger |
| `RG-2 complex restoration` | nerves, compound bone, localized organs, digits/peripheral structures | controlled rest; sometimes medical guidance | days/weeks + substrate |
| `RG-3 major reconstruction` | major organ loss, hand/foot, limb, extensive burn/crush | protected reconstruction | weeks/months, sanctuary, specialists |
| `RG-4 central rescue` | partial brain/spinal damage, multi-organ collapse | emergency stabilization + apex regeneration | seconds/minutes to stabilize; high mortality |
| `RG-5 continuity reconstruction` | body lost but a hypothetical human continuity-Core survives | legendary/exceptional only | years, enormous resources; H2 fork |

These are backstage test bands, not necessarily names spoken in the story.

## 3. What determines the ceiling

### Inherited/input factors
- donor species and developmental architecture;
- quality, integrity and quantity of the granted Matrix material;
- ancestral/Primordial potency where applicable;
- compatibility with the recipient;
- inherited human biology and prior damage.

### Achieved factors
- ritual success and Mark integration;
- years of reciprocal development;
- training in regulating growth and Quintessence;
- current maturity of the generated Beast relationship;
- medical/alchemical knowledge;
- available reserve and environmental support.

Ancestral blood may raise the ceiling and improve efficiency, but does not guarantee attainment. A compatible, disciplined recipient of less exalted material may outperform an unstable heir carrying more prestigious blood.

## 4. Automatic healing versus reconstructive regeneration

**Automatic response:** closes vessels, prevents infection/bleeding, restores skin and muscle and begins bone repair while the person remains active. It can be visibly fast at higher development.

**Reconstructive response:** regrows missing geometry. The organism must create a regenerative zone, protect it, obtain substrate and rebuild in sequence. The fighter is often removed from combat. Meditation may help control and Resonance; it does not replace matter.

Human form is usually the efficient recovery configuration for an autonomous Beast because it reduces the quantity of tissue being maintained and makes sanctuary/logistics easier. This does not make humanoid form metaphysically superior.

## 5. Brain injury and true-death threshold

Avoid the binary rule `brain touched = dead`. At high regenerative development:

- penetration or a localized slice can be survivable if circulation, enough neural/control architecture and identity continuity remain;
- partial tissue loss can be stabilized and rebuilt;
- rapid self-repair may begin before ordinary irreversible cascades complete;
- the Beast/partner or healer can temporarily sustain circulation, pattern and control.

Death becomes likely when damage destroys the distributed systems needed to preserve the person faster than they can stabilize. Complete destruction of the brain/central continuity network remains true death for H0/H1 humans. H2 only changes this if a separate mature continuity-Core actually exists and survives.

Therefore:

> **A wound can be medically catastrophic without being ontologically final; true death occurs when continuity can no longer preserve or reconstruct the person.**

## 6. Regeneration blockers

Use mechanisms rather than a generic `anti-heal` debuff:

- **pattern severance:** the wound disrupts positional/body information;
- **resonant interference:** control signals cannot coordinate repair;
- **foreign signature/corruption:** new growth follows a hostile pattern;
- **fixation:** tissue is forced into scar, crystal, ash, necrosis or another stable non-regenerative state;
- **resource denial:** blood flow, oxygen-equivalent, substrate or Quintessence is cut off;
- **damage outruns repair:** continuous fire, poison, crushing or repeated injury consumes the regenerative margin;
- **central interruption:** the control network is destroyed before a partner/healer can stabilize it.

Different weapons, curses and species can exploit different blockers. No universal anti-regeneration technology should be cheap or ubiquitous.

## 7. Role of medicine and alchemy

Elite healers do more than pour energy into a patient. They may:

- preserve circulation and consciousness;
- remove hostile signatures/toxins;
- restore or externally hold the body-pattern;
- align bone, nerve and organ geometry;
- prevent premature scarring/malformed growth;
- provide biomaterial, minerals and Quintessence;
- coordinate the partner Beast’s support;
- maintain a sanctuary field during long reconstruction.

This produces legitimate medicine, surgery, alchemy, rehabilitation and battlefield triage even in a highly regenerative society.

## 8. Costs without a mana bar

Regeneration does not require a numeric pool visible to readers. Cost is shown through consequences:

- sudden weakness or loss of peak technique;
- heat, tremor, hunger, dehydration and sensory dulling;
- forced reversion to efficient form;
- inability to externalize a field body;
- long sleep, protected meditation or hospitalization;
- consumption of rare food/minerals/biomass;
- vulnerability while patterning tissue;
- slower or malformed repair if forced repeatedly.

The causal variables remain backstage. The scene shows blood stopping, bone moving, the character losing balance, the healer ordering substrate and the fighter realizing the arm will take months—not a blue bar falling from 43 to 12.

## 9. Autonomous Beast projection

After birth, a Beast can allocate a finite reserve to a remote field avatar. This avatar can be injured/destroyed as a field manifestation. The biological organism remains the continuity-bearing person. Projection competes with regeneration and other high-cost work; sending a powerful avatar while healing a limb may be impossible or reckless.
