The author repeatedly frames complete maturation as reaching the **capacity** to become autonomous and asks whether separation is facultative. A mature host-protected Beast might delay definitive incarnation to continue developing/learning under protected continuity.

This does not overturn definitive biological birth. It reopens the timing question:

`maturity threshold → capable of birth` does not necessarily equal `instant compulsory birth`.

**Status:** OPEN FOR CE-05. Do not present either mandatory immediate birth or indefinite protected immortality as settled.

**Status:** STRONG DIRECTION / NEEDS CE-05 STRESS-TEST. This is not a universal rule for every species yet.
