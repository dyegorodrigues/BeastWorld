> This record preserves the author’s 01/09/2026 corrections and questions. It supersedes any CE-02 example that implied a fully corporeal Beast could be casually externalized by an ordinary fighter and make the host rapidly exhausted merely by remaining linked.

## 1. Author corrections preserved

### A. Manifestation state must be explicit
The cost, fatigue and combat implications depend on the Beast’s embodiment state. The author distinguishes, without yet canonizing a universal ladder:
- light / transparent spectral manifestation;
- denser spectral manifestation;
- increasingly substantial bestial or humanoid / Magic Beast Knight manifestation;
- fully corporeal / materially consolidated manifestation.

A fighter capable of full corporeal manifestation is not an ordinary novice by default. Full manifestation may require preparation, maturity, high reserve/coherence and can represent a major expenditure. Different size/density/morphology choices can optimize cost.

### B. Reintegration can restore function
The author rejects the overly restrictive reading that recall only stops further cost. If the Beast returns with substantial remaining capacity, reintegration can restore the human’s integrated functional state **in proportion to the Beast’s condition and remaining resources**.

Working interpretation to stress-test:
- strongest restoration: magical/resonant exhaustion, loss of coherence, overextension caused by split operation;
- substantial but non-infinite restoration may extend to ordinary physiological/neuromuscular fatigue because the coupled organism is reconstituted and resource/support flow is reorganized;
- cognitive overload from operating two viewpoints can ease sharply when the system returns to one integrated sensorimotor loop;
- structural trauma (fracture, hemorrhage, severe organ injury, concussion) is NOT automatically erased; regeneration remains a separate costly process.

Recall therefore can be a tactical **recovery/concentration maneuver**, not a free refill: the Beast loses its separate battlefield body and a later re-externalization pays formation cost again.

### C. Externalization does not strip the human’s permanent bestial adaptations
The human retains Mark/Blood/interface-derived senses, instincts, physical amplification potential and magical access while the Beast is external. Full internal integration can add deeper co-processing/synchronization, but externalization does not make the host a baseline human.

### D. Mere connection should not be a constant major energy tax
The bond itself may be persistent at low cost. Major costs should arise from:
- creating/densifying/materializing a second body;
- supporting it when its own reserve/ambient intake is insufficient;
- active high-bandwidth synchronization/shared senses;
- simultaneous precision combat with two bodies;
- long-distance transfer/support;
- repair/reconstruction.

This replaces the simplistic assumption that simply keeping the Beast outside continuously drains the host at a large rate.

### E. Two-body cognition is a real training axis
The author strongly supports the tactical value and cost of processing two bodies/two visual fields simultaneously. Training can increase automaticity, attention switching and shared sensorimotor integration. This can distinguish elite pairs without creating a numerical stat ladder.

### F. Recall as preservation
If the host is incapacitated and the external Beast is still developmentally anchored, recall may be the safest action to preserve both identities and avoid catastrophic external destruction / soul-Mark shock. A mature Beast may choose otherwise if it can maintain itself.

### G. Developmental anchoring versus completed autonomous life
Author direction under stress-test:
- while the generated Beast is still completing its developmental cycle, the human link gives it a major advantage: an external body can be recalled/reconstituted while identity/core continuity remains protected;
- as maturation proceeds, the Beast develops a fully stable core/identity and increasingly independent metabolism/resonance;
- if the human dies before this cycle is sufficiently complete, survival may be possible but the Beast can emerge weakened, unstable or developmentally compromised; below some threshold it may collapse with the host;
- after full autonomous maturation, the Beast can persist as a true independent person. The exact point at which external-body death becomes ordinary true death versus recall-eligible remains OPEN for the regeneration/true-death gate.

### H. Natural Beasts, generated Beasts and voluntary partnerships are not the same path
Preserve at least three possible relationship classes for future gates:
1. **natural magical Beast** — born as an autonomous organism in the ecology;
2. **generated / co-developed Beast** — new life emerging through human + Matrix/Mark + Living World development;
3. **voluntary human–existing Beast bond** — an already autonomous Beast forms a relationship with a human; it may grant blood/material and share resonance, but this is not automatically the same process as generating a new Beast Soul.

Do not collapse these into one summon/bond system.

### I. Reciprocal evolution remains central
Generated Beasts represent a new branch of Beast life shaped by human culture, martial technique, language and cognition while humans undergo their own magical/biological evolution through the same coupling. Mature survivors can become ancestral persons, reproduce, bond, live socially and potentially establish new clades. This is reciprocal ecological evolution, not one species exploiting a power source.

### J. Feeding direction must be preserved, not overwritten
The author recalls prior work in which Beasts can eat normally, while sufficiently mature/high-coherence beings may reach a state where ordinary feeding becomes optional or unnecessary because environmental/Quintessence sustain is sufficient. Exact threshold/mechanism is not re-decided here. Existing natural-Beast ecology still requires species-level diet and habitat in its own layer; do not silently apply post-symbiotic high-order autonomy to all wild animals.

## 2. Revised visual model for CE-02

A fighter should not be described as simply “summoning a Beast.” The viewer must know roughly **how much of the Beast is being made physically present**.

Example continuum for stress-test only:
- a translucent raptor-form scouts and shares senses with relatively low embodiment cost;
- a dense spectral raptor can strike, deflect or carry briefly at higher cost;
- a near-corporeal / corporeal Phoenix can bleed, collide with masonry, carry a rider and fight independently, but requires substantially more formation and material organization;
- the same mature Phoenix may compress into a smaller bestial or humanoid warrior-form for lower mass/greater fine control;
- colossal form provides transport, force and territorial dominance but is not the cheapest default combat configuration.

These are **configurations**, not universal levels.

## 3. Revised reintegration hypothesis

Working image:

`split pair → one partner overextended → recall → resources + sensory loops + resonance re-concentrate → integrated combat state partially/strongly restored → re-externalization later costs again.`

This creates a tactical cycle rather than a mana refill.

Key constraint: recovery is bounded by what remains in the coupled pair and by actual bodily damage. No lifespan sacrifice, essence-burning or mandatory vitality-loss mechanic is introduced.

## 4. External red-team implications

Research on exercise fatigue supports keeping the reader-facing experience integrated while allowing multiple backstage causes: fatigue is systemic and can include muscular, neural, cognitive and perceptual components. This does **not** require characters to narrate separate gauges.

Dual-task literature supports the plausibility of training attention allocation/automaticity for simultaneous perception and action. Symbiosis literature supports bidirectional exchange through regulated interfaces rather than an instantly shared reservoir.

External science remains analogy only; supernatural coherence can legitimately allow recovery and embodiment behaviors beyond real physiology.

## 5. Terminology correction

`Fôlego` is no longer treated as preferred final in-world terminology. The author finds it too mundane/non-martial and translation-poor. Until the later naming gate, use **integrated combat capacity / exhaustion state** backstage and avoid implying that `Fôlego` is settled lore.
