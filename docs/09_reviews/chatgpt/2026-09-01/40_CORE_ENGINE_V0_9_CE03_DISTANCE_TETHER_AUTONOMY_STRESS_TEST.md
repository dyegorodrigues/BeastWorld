> Reader-facing question: when a Beast crosses a courtyard, then a city, then a mountain range, what changes first? Avoid hard RPG radius and avoid a permanent high energy tax merely for being connected.

## 1. Competing implementations

### R1 — Hard radius tether
At a defined distance the Beast loses stability/returns/disappears.

**Result:** reject as universal rule. It is game-like, confuses relationship with leash and contradicts mature autonomy.

### R2 — Layered bandwidth + developmental anchoring
Distance progressively reduces what the pair can do **together** before it threatens existence. High-bandwidth shared perception and precision coordination degrade first; active support/transfer degrades next; coarse affective/intent communication may persist farther. Existential dependence is separate and applies only while a generated Beast’s continuity/Core remains developmentally host-protected.

**Result:** laboratory leader.

### R3 — Infinite nonlocal bond
Perfect senses, communication and transfer at arbitrary distance.

**Result:** reject as universal rule because it creates omniscience, surveillance, trivial logistics and infinite-support exploits. Rare exceptional links may be reconsidered later.

## 2. Two different links

### Tactical bond
Exists in generated and voluntary bonded pairs. It carries shared senses, intention, coordination, active support/transfer and Convergence synchronization. It is high-bandwidth and sensitive to separation, interference and divided attention.

### Developmental existential root
Exists only while a generated Beast’s continuity/Core remains protected through the human. It maintains continuity between the protected identity and an external field manifestation. It is not constant telepathy and should be harder to disrupt than tactical bandwidth.

At autonomous birth, the continuity/Core becomes self-housed in the Beast’s definitive biological body. The relationship remains; the umbilical requirement ends.

## 3. Default degradation order

1. micro-synchrony weakens;
2. high-definition shared senses become intermittent/coarse;
3. active support and large transfers become inefficient;
4. communication simplifies toward impulse, emotion, direction, danger and recognition;
5. only a still-host-anchored generated Beast faces existential danger under much stronger separation/interference;
6. natural and autonomously born Beasts remain alive regardless of human distance.

No universal metres are promoted. Training, maturity, barriers, environment and Living-World conditions change the curve.

## 4. Manifestation/body state

- **light field manifestation:** economical but may depend more on remote pattern support while young;
- **dense field body:** carries greater local reserve/processing and can fight independently for longer;
- **remote field avatar of an autonomous Beast:** operates from a pre-allocated finite reserve while the true biological body remains elsewhere;
- **autonomous biological body:** has no existential range limit to the formative human; only relationship bandwidth is range-sensitive.

There is no ordinary mixed body or temporary biological soma in this progression.

## 5. Stress-tests

### ST-1 — Young generated Phoenix across an academy courtyard
She scouts/strikes as a field manifestation. Detailed shared vision degrades before her manifestation does; a novice may deliberately close the channel because of cognitive overload.

**PASS.**

### ST-2 — Developed host-anchored Phoenix crosses a city
She sustains a dense field body with local reserve. The human does not pay a major continuous tax merely because she is distant. Perfect synchrony fades; full sensory feed becomes coarse awareness. Catastrophic damage far away makes recovery harder because direct stabilization is unavailable, but the developing identity remains host-protected.

**PASS strong.**

### ST-3 — Host becomes unconscious
A young field manifestation may recall automatically or by instinct. A more developed host-anchored Beast can continue local defense. An autonomous Beast continues normally and can physically evacuate the human.

**PASS.**

### ST-4 — Autonomous ancestral Phoenix leaves the Dynasty
Her Core and biological body are autonomous. She can leave for years. The old relationship cannot function as perfect continental GPS/telepathy; deliberate contact or proximity can restore bandwidth.

**PASS.**

### ST-5 — Natural Dragon bonded to a rider
The Dragon was autonomous before the bond. Distance weakens shared senses/intent but cannot dismantle the Dragon.

**PASS.**

### ST-6 — Mounted long-distance travel
Distance from the departure point is irrelevant; separation **between partners** matters. Because rider and Beast remain together, tactical bandwidth may stay excellent.

**PASS.**

### ST-7 — Anti-resonance barrier
A specialized barrier can cut tactical bandwidth at close range. Both persons initially remain alive. For a still-host-anchored generated Beast outside in field form, prolonged isolation may prevent safe recall/stabilization and become dangerous.

**PASS strong; specialized story tool.**

### ST-8 — Destruction far from host
Destruction of a host-anchored field body causes shock and expensive reconstitution, not an ordinary corpse or automatic death. Destruction of an autonomous Core-bearing biological body threatens true death under CE-04 rules.

**PASS.**

### ST-9 — Autonomous Phoenix sends a remote projection
The Phoenix remains bodily at a sanctuary and invests a bounded reserve in a humanoid or bestial field avatar sent into the city. It can act until that allocated reserve, pattern stability or communication is exhausted. Destroying the avatar wounds her reserve/feedback, not her organism.

**PASS provisional; exact range and autonomy remain open.**

## 6. Guardrails

- no universal radius;
- no permanent continental GPS/telepathy;
- no distance tax merely for existing;
- no natural/autonomous Beast death from loss of human signal;
- no `spectral = young` or `humanoid = superior`;
- no mixed/temporary biological body;
- no ubiquitous anti-resonance pet-disabler;
- no remote avatar as free duplicate or infinite summon.
