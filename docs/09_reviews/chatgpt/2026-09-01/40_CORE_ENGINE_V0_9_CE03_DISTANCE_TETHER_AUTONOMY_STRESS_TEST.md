**Data:** 01/09/2026

> Reader-facing question: when a Beast crosses a courtyard, then a city, then a mountain range, what actually changes first? Avoid hard RPG radius and avoid a permanent high energy tax just for being connected.

## 1. Three competing implementations

### R1 — Hard radius tether
At a defined distance the Beast loses stability/returns/disappears.

**Pros:** simple.  
**Fails:** game-like; contradicts mature autonomy; makes travel/ancestral life awkward; confuses relationship with leash.  
**Result:** reject as universal rule.

### R2 — Layered bandwidth + developmental anchoring
Distance progressively reduces what the pair can do **together** before it threatens the Beast’s existence. High-bandwidth shared perception and precision coordination degrade earlier; active resource transfer/support degrades next; coarse affective/intent communication can persist farther. Existential dependence is separate and depends on whether the generated Beast’s Core remains host-anchored.

**Result:** laboratory leader.

### R3 — Infinite nonlocal bond
Perfect senses, communication and transfer at arbitrary distance.

**Pros:** emotionally attractive; simple.  
**Fails:** omniscience, surveillance, no separation stakes, trivial logistics, weak geography, potential infinite support exploits.  
**Result:** reject as universal rule; rare exceptional cosmological links can be reconsidered later.

## 2. Key architecture — two different links

### A. Tactical bond
Exists in both generated and voluntary bonded pairs.
Functions:
- shared senses;
- intention/communication;
- coordination;
- active support/transfer;
- deep Convergence synchronization.

This is high-bandwidth and most sensitive to separation, interference and divided attention.

### B. Existential root
Relevant specifically to a **generated Beast whose mature Core/identity is still developmentally protected through the human**.

It maintains continuity between the protected Core/pattern and external soma. It is not a conversational channel and need not carry detailed senses. It should be harder to disrupt than tactical bandwidth.

At final autonomous maturation, this existential root relocates/completes into the Beast’s own Core/body. The human relationship can remain, but it is no longer an umbilical requirement.

Natural Beasts and already-autonomous mature generated Beasts do not require an existential root to a human.

## 3. Reader-facing degradation order — leader hypothesis

For an external Beast moving away from its human, the default order should be approximately:

1. **micro-synchrony weakens first** — perfect split-second co-combat and continuous shared sensorimotor fusion becomes harder;
2. **high-definition shared senses degrade** — full visual/auditory stream becomes intermittent or coarse;
3. **active support/large transfer becomes inefficient** — sending substantial reserve/stabilization across separation is harder;
4. **communication simplifies** — words/images may become impulses, emotions, direction, danger, recognition;
5. **existential continuity is affected only for still-host-anchored generated Beasts**, and only at much stronger separation/interference than tactical effects;
6. **autonomous mature/natural Beasts remain fully alive regardless of human range.**

No exact metres are promoted. Environment, maturity, training, Living-World coherence, architecture/barriers and special interference can alter the curve.

## 4. Manifestation state matters

Distance does not affect every body equally.

- **light spectral projection:** low mass but often more dependent on coherent remote patterning; may lose definition when too far from host if the Beast is young;
- **dense spectral body:** can carry more local reserve and local processing; better independent operation but still benefits from tactical bandwidth;
- **mixed/alchemical temporary soma:** recruited local matter gives mechanical persistence and lowers some continuous embodiment demands; distance primarily reduces shared cognition/support rather than making the soma evaporate;
- **autonomous Core-bearing living body:** no existential range limit to the original human; only relationship bandwidth is range-sensitive.

This prevents `farther = automatically more mana per second`.

## 5. Stress-test scenes

### ST-1 — Young generated Phoenix across an academy courtyard
The Phoenix can scout/strike spectrally. At close range the student partially shares sight. As she moves farther, detailed visual overlay becomes distracting/faint before the body itself destabilizes. If the student is novice, cognitive overload may force him to drop shared vision deliberately.

**PASS.**

### ST-2 — Developed host-anchored Phoenix crosses a city
She has enough local reserve and a dense/mixed soma to fly independently. The human does not pay continuous major energy merely because she is away. Their perfect combat synchrony fades; full visual feed gives way to coarse awareness/intent. If she is badly damaged far away, recall/reconstruction is more dangerous because high-bandwidth stabilization is unavailable, but the protected identity anchor remains with the host.

**PASS strong.**

### ST-3 — Host falls unconscious
A young external Beast may autonomously choose recall because high-level support disappeared and its existential anchor is vulnerable. A more mature host-anchored Beast can continue local defense. An autonomous Beast can continue normally and physically evacuate/protect the human.

**PASS.**

### ST-4 — Ancestral generated Phoenix leaves the Dynasty
Her Core and body are autonomous. She can leave for years. The old bond with descendants/partners cannot function as constant GPS/telepathy. Proximity or deliberate resonant contact can restore high bandwidth when relationships are renewed.

**PASS; protects personhood and politics.**

### ST-5 — Natural Dragon bonded to a rider
The Dragon was autonomous before the bond. Distance can weaken shared senses/intent but never dismantles the Dragon. If rider and Dragon separate for a season, both remain whole persons.

**PASS; clearly distinct from generated ontogeny.**

### ST-6 — Long-distance mounted travel
Because rider and Beast remain physically close, tactical bandwidth can remain excellent even across hundreds of kilometres traveled. Distance from the departure point is irrelevant; separation **between partners** is what matters. The Beast uses own metabolism/reserve/ambient sustain for travel.

**PASS; avoids map-coordinate game logic.**

### ST-7 — hostile anti-resonance barrier
A specialized field/barrier can disrupt tactical bandwidth even at ten metres. Shared senses vanish, transfer stops, but both persons initially remain alive. For a still-host-anchored generated Beast with external soma, prolonged disruption can prevent safe recall/stabilization and become genuinely dangerous. This creates a tactical counter without a universal instant-kill switch.

**PASS strong; valuable story tool, must remain specialized.**

### ST-8 — body destruction far from host
If host-anchored identity Core remains protected, distant soma destruction creates severe shock, information/pattern loss and expensive reconstruction but not automatic death. If the autonomous Core is in the Beast’s own body, destruction threatens true death according to later Core rules.

**PASS; connects to future regeneration gate.**

## 6. Core/maturation interaction

The author’s new `grain → growing Core → mature autonomous Core` intuition can fit CE-03 if treated as a developmental condensation, not as a mandatory RPG crystal from birth.

Promising sequence to test later:
- distributed identity/germinal pattern;
- micro-condensate/seed forms while host-protected;
- Core matures and carries increasing self-stability;
- external somata become progressively less host-dependent;
- final autonomous incarnation places the mature Core in/with Beast’s own stable body.

Until final completion, the protected host anchoring is an advantage (reconstruction/recall) and a dependency (cannot simply abandon the relationship without developmental cost).

## 7. Social/emotional consequence

Generated Beasts often experience the formative human as more than a contractual partner: the human is part parent, developmental environment, teacher and first family. This gives a strong non-coercive reason for them to remain near the human even as physical autonomy grows. Autonomy therefore does not imply an immediate desire to leave.

Do not universalize temperament: some relationships can be conflicted, abusive, estranged or politically complicated.

## 8. Red-team guardrails

- no exact universal radius;
- no constant GPS/telepathy across continents;
- no distance mana tax merely for existing;
- no natural/autonomous Beast death from losing a human signal;
- no hard assumption that spectral = young or corporeal = mature;
- no collapse of voluntary natural-Beast bonds into generated-Beast developmental anchoring;
- no anti-resonance technology as ubiquitous easy pet-disabler.
