## A. Details requiring explicit preservation

1. **Autonomous reserve / rescue function.** An external Beast Soul can retain its own usable reserve. Reintegration may let the paired human access what remains, functioning as an emergency resource, but must not create free energy or instant 100% restoration.
2. **Range/tether.** Externalized Beast Souls remain linked over a finite coherence/communication range that can expand with mastery, environment and compatibility. Range should be stress-tested rather than treated as arbitrary radius.
3. **Persistent phenotype.** The blood/Mark ritual can produce subtle stable human changes (iris, hair, pigmentation, presence) without animal muzzle/tail/wings. These can become visual lineage/Mark language.
4. **Several external morphologies.** The PDF implies at least: spectral proto-form; external bestial body; external humanoid/warrior spectral or incarnate body; mature social/compressed humanoid body; and host Convergence War-Body. These must not be collapsed into one transformation ladder.
5. **Visual maturation choreography.** Early manifestation may begin as light/mist/formless presence, then gain silhouette, density and solidity. Host war-body may assemble regionally and the helmet closure can be a strong cinematic completion cue without equalling a universal rank.
6. **Forced recall consequence.** If the external body is destroyed and recalled for reconstruction, high-order fusion/War-Body should be unavailable or impaired until identity/coherence recovers.
7. **Ambient sustain.** A developed external Beast may draw compatible local Quintessence and therefore persist more cheaply in favorable ecologies/Sanctuaries; this connects combat to geography.
8. **Bestial-body cost may differ from humanoid-warrior embodiment.** Different morphologies should have different embodiment/materialization costs and affordances rather than one generic summon cost.
9. **Roaming/experience.** Externalization exists not only for combat: the Beast can live, observe, explore and accumulate experiences while paired, within tether constraints.
10. **Mount role.** Some large external Beasts can function as mounts/transport if consensual and contextually appropriate; because they are persons, this is cooperation, not ownership.
11. **Post-host soul residue.** The author speculated that a mature Beast may carry a fragment/echo of its deceased human partner's essence. Preserve as late cosmology hypothesis only; do not yet promote resurrection, possession or afterlife mechanics.
12. **Reproduction/new clades.** Mature generated Beasts may reproduce in nature. This creates a major feedback loop: human-assisted Beast → free organism → descendants/new magical lineages → future matrices. Requires ecology/heredity stress-test.
13. **Ancestral House patrons.** Long-lived former partner-Beasts may stay near a Dynasty without re-pairing, voluntarily grant matrix material at rare moments, advise, witness history, or leave.
14. **Epistemic stratification around Primordials.** High elites may know Primordials as Living-Star manifestations/persons while lower strata mythologize them as gods; Primordial avatars may walk among people unrecognized.
15. **Distributed-star consciousness.** The author proposed the Living Star's total awareness as abstract, with Primordials acting as distinct sapient consciousnesses/pillars of a whole. Promising but major metaphysical gate; number four/five remains open.
16. **Ritual music as civilizational ecology.** Collective song, rhythm, breath and acoustic space may improve coherence/Quintessence flow and be pleasurable/meaningful to Beast life, not merely cast a spell.
17. **Human–mature-Beast relationships.** The author explicitly explored romance/sexual partnership between adult humans and fully sapient humanoid-form Beasts. Preserve as social/biological possibility to stress-test; do not assume reproduction automatically produces human children. Consent, personhood, species biology and lineage implications require a separate gate.
18. **Early childhood Mark remains viable.** The author prefers privileged Houses to begin biological/Mark preparation early because development takes years. Avoid collision with `Spirit Animals` by decoupling early Mark from universal coming-of-age summoning: modification can begin early while germination may never occur, occur later, or require different triggers.
19. **Marked non-germinators.** People who never form an autonomous Beast still retain magical/biological benefits and can become formidable; some may germinate late or unlock alternative paths. Essential for power diversity.
20. **House purity ideology.** Dragon Houses may culturally favor draconic compatibility and avoid mixing to preserve inherited Mark tendencies, but 'purity' must not be an objective linear power score. Admixture may cause incompatibility, neutrality or novel emergent compatibility. This is a story/politics generator, not a scientific truth.
21. **Common vs rare visual families.** Common matrices may produce more standardized morphologies useful for military/social readability, while rare/ancestral matrices and unusual hosts produce highly individualized forms.
22. **Sentient-body material economy.** Beast remains/tissues can support artifacts, weapons, armor, clothing, medicine/elixirs. This immediately raises consent, corpse rights, hunting, salvage and black-market law.
23. **Same ritual physics can later accept non-bestial matrices.** Demonic/infernal, angelic/celestial, monstrous or foreign-world substrates can become late-saga extensions of one engine rather than separate power systems. Keep out of Book 1 until local identity is stable.
24. **Martial culture predates magic.** Human weapon schools and body disciplines existed before the founder rituals; later magical Forms should grow from those pre-existing traditions, preserving martial causality.
25. **Director moral edge.** Beyond charm/flirting and love of challenge, the author described rational exhilaration under violence, willingness to remove restraints, and possible acceptance of collateral/sacrifice to neutralize a threat. Must be calibrated so he remains intelligent and narratively legible rather than becoming a Gojo/Kenpachi imitation or casual sociopath.
26. **Rejected throwaway:** a speculative hidden `destruction/light` resonance for the great Dragon ancestor was explicitly abandoned in the brainstorm (`forget it`). Do not propagate it as active hypothesis unless the author reopens it.

## B. Web collision reminder

Official `Spirit Animals` material confirms a coming-of-age ritual, spirit-animal bond and powers shared by human and animal, and passive tattoo-state imagery. Therefore BeastWorld's early childhood Mark should not be staged as a universal ceremony that instantly summons a destined animal. The differentiator remains reciprocal organism generation, Living-World ecology, autonomous maturation, post-host life and martial/weapon grammar.

`Solo Leveling` remains useful for kinetic/visual reference, but its action director's stated principle of extreme speed remaining perceptible reinforces BeastWorld's requirement that high-speed combat stay readable rather than becoming teleport cuts.
