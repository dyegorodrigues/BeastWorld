## 2. Political architecture — preferred prototype

### Dynasty

The founding Dynasty is both:

- the ruling lineage descended from a Primordial-chosen founder; and
- the historical nucleus around which a territorial State formed.

It governs a population far larger than its bloodline: ordinary humans, cities, provinces, lesser houses, guilds, military forces, courts, archives and infrastructure.

### Sovereign

One person holds the human office of rule. Final title is open. The sovereign governs people, law, revenue, war and administration. The sovereign does not own or command the Primordial.

### `Crown`

Retain only as backstage/comparative vocabulary for the sovereignty of a Dynasty-State. Do not treat it as a separate central government unless a later fork deliberately creates one.

### `Seat`

Retire as an independent third jurisdiction in the preferred prototype. If the word survives, it means the office/place/voice of a Dynasty's sovereignty or its seat in the common pact. It does not create a parallel ecological state.

### Primordial pact

The relationship with the Primordial precedes and exceeds human law. It can legitimize, embarrass or destabilize a ruler, but it does not directly issue a human succession decree.

### Inter-dynastic pact

The Dynasties retain sovereignty. A limited common compact handles only problems that cannot be solved separately: the Academy, selected joint military doctrine, defined inter-dynastic emergencies, Fends when appropriate and treaty arbitration. It does not tax or police everyday life.

### Academy

The Academy is an autonomous institution founded and sustained by treaty. It is not a federal capital and does not replace each Dynasty's own military, administrative or martial schools.

## 3. What happened at the founding — coherent history envelope

### Strong working sequence

1. A civilizational/ecological crisis creates a need for human access to powers they cannot yet wield safely.
2. Primordials choose one or a few human founders for reasons not reducible to hereditary law.
3. The chosen receive a direct biological-mystical concession and foundational knowledge around Matrix, compatibility, voluntary Beast material and disciplined use of the World's power.
4. Their descendants inherit recombined and variable architecture, not a perfect copy.
5. Over centuries, each lineage accumulates archives, Sanctuaries, training, alliances, Beast relations and coercive control over access; a Dynasty-State forms around that advantage.
6. Later Beast-blood practices broaden the empowered elite without making direct Primordial concession routine.

Exact crisis, number of founders, knowledge transmitted and timing remain open.

## 4. Three distinct blood layers

Avoid a universal `purity percentage`.

| Layer | Function | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| hereditary founder architecture | variable biological-mystical inheritance | recombines; advantage is not mastery |
| voluntary/processed Beast material | can strengthen or alter an existing organism under rules still open | cannot reproduce direct Primordial concession by accumulation |
| direct Primordial concession | living-source qualitative rewrite/expansion | rare, sovereign, no automatic office or learned technique |

People inside the world may use language of `pure blood` as ideology or shorthand. The engine should track concrete consequences: tolerance, capacity, stability, compatibility, recovery, possible ceiling and inheritance risk.

Historical dynasties often planned succession horizontally through brothers, cousins and nephews as well as vertically, which supports this pressure without requiring an incompetent heir: https://academic.oup.com/past/article/256/1/87/6491208 and https://academic.oup.com/bics/advance-article/doi/10.1093/bics/qbag037/8748314

## 6. Why the direct concession matters

The Director is politically dangerous not merely because he is stronger.

- He may represent the first direct Dragon concession since the founding or in many centuries — exact rarity open.
- He proves that the Primordial's agency did not become hereditary property of the main line.
- His body may constitute a new high-capacity dynastic root: a possible `second founder` in the eyes of jurists, priests, strategists and marriage planners.
- His descendants could become a rival lineage even if he never makes a claim.
- Enemies can support him without his consent; allies can demand that he protect the State by accepting office; the principal line can fear what others will do with his existence.

The Primordial did not choose a king. Human beings create the succession crisis around an act whose original meaning may be different.

## 8. Military function — command without permanent sovereignty

The Director is neither a lone wolf incapable of cooperation nor a permanent supreme general.

### Strategic/operational level

- helps design campaigns, doctrine and mission objectives;
- can receive temporary authority over a joint force or theatre when competence and threat justify it;
- coordinates capable commanders and delegates rather than micromanaging every duel;
- returns extraordinary command when the mandate ends.

### Tactical level

- often operates as an autonomous rupture element at the most dangerous point;
- prefers to fight alone when weaker allies would consume attention, restrict force or become collateral victims;
- works exceptionally well with peers who can read the battle, survive his operating envelope and create real advantage.

Modern joint-command structures demonstrate the functional principle: sovereign members can retain national forces while transferring defined operational authority for a mission. This is an analogy, not a lore template: https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/organization/nato-structure/allied-command-transformation and https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/organization/nato-structure/allied-command-operations-aco

## 10. Book 1 axis — corrected label

The selected axis is no longer `the Crown` as a separate institution.

> **Book 1 axis: fractured dynastic legitimacy.**

Three forms of legitimacy stop aligning:

1. **law and principal-line succession** — supports the competent cousin;
2. **direct living concession** — marks the Director without naming him ruler;
3. **demonstrated authority and public confidence** — accumulate around what the Director actually does.

The Director's desire is to remain free to lead, teach, fight and protect without becoming sovereign. The system does not need him to want the throne; other people can turn him into a claimant.

### Irreversible Book 1 direction to test

At the beginning, refusal and institutional neutrality still seem possible. By the end, the Director's actions have made neutrality impossible: accepting rule would imprison him, but refusing it no longer prevents a political movement from existing in his name.

This is a narrative hypothesis, not a fixed plot.
