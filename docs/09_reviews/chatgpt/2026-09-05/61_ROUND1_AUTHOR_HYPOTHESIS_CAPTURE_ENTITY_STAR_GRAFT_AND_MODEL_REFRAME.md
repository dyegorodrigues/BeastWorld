## 3. New authorial hypotheses preserved
### Deep Entities as civilizational gods in experience
The author wants them to be almost godlike relative to civilization while retaining the metabiological construction: persons integrated with Living World/Quintessence, capable of ritual/statue/sanctuary representation and bounded material embodiment.

### Entity–human communication
Possible resonant/internal communication can provide warnings, impressions, advice or dialogue without becoming a literal game System or AI assistant.

### Primordial material and protagonist
Preserved biological/primordial material associated with a Deep Entity remains a possible layer in the protagonist's history. Must not be automatically collapsed into Blood, Matrix, Concession and communication.

### Sacrificial transmutation
High-value integration candidate:
Deep Entities use their original bodies as living components in a world-scale Quintessence/alchemical transmutation that opens a Fissure and transfers real ecological continuity out of the dying Beast World.

This could causally unify:
- death of original Entity bodies;
- remnant formation;
- Fissures;
- alchemy;
- preserved old-world material;
- ruins/resources;
- later graft.

### Travel chronology
Author preference shifts away from a literal three-thousand-year physical voyage. Stronger candidate:
`catastrophe → Fissure transfer → graft → long post-graft coevolution → protagonist era`.
Exact centuries/millennia remain open.

### Host `estrela`
The destination is imagined as life-rich but developmentally young in world-consciousness/Quintessence integration. `Estrela` remains an authorial ambiguous term and must not be normalized prematurely into astronomical star, planet or world-core.

### Resonant destination
A young host's intense/clean Quintessence or vital potential may provide a compatible anchor for the escaping remnant.

### Graft consequences
The graft remains comparatively harmonious at immediate physical scale yet enormous over generations: transformed flora/fauna, new bestial lineages, materials, ruins/resources, alchemy, medicine, martial-magic traditions, Blood/Matrix and institutions.

### Non-Dynastic culture
Preserve a deferred possibility of a region/continent that shares the transformed world but develops a different shamanic/magical/hunting/ecological civilizational path rather than the same Entity–Dynasty structure.
