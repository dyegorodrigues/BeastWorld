## 1. Why this pass exists
Pass 55 successfully reconstructed a causal architecture from the author’s large origin brainstorm but left the route explicitly waiting for validation. The author has now validated the structural direction and asked for two additional corrections:

1. promote the reviewed route so the project can continue along it; and
2. repair the Notion experience, which had become a mixture of current material, old V3/V4 wiki pages, laboratories, raw source and governance artifacts that were safe archivally but confusing to read as a human.

The author also demanded a final nuance audit of the original brainstorm and the two recent explanatory reports, specifically to prevent small ideas, emotional intent and unasked-but-useful implications from being lost by technical compression.

## 2. Promotion boundary
The structural route is now `WORKING-CANON`, not final `CANON`.

Promoted:
- older Beast World → near-extinction → survival remnant → graft into initially non-magical human world → present composite BeastWorld;
- reciprocal human–Beast/world development across millennia;
- old predator eventually finds the refuge again;
- main protagonist route beginning around age fifteen and earning the adult Director;
- Direct Concession during the first coordinated Fissure campaign as the current Book 1 axis;
- cousin as legitimate designated successor and independently formidable combatant;
- reciprocal versus forced/extractive incorporation as current structural thematic opposition.

Not auto-promoted:
- exact remnant physics/name;
- exact entity count/domains;
- exact Blood/Matrix/Concession mechanism;
- exact maternal/Oceanic genealogy;
- exact manifestation of Concession;
- exact Dynastic map/titles;
- exact interworld integration ethics/mechanics;
- exact romantic configuration;
- concrete Book 1 incident, champion, climax or scenes.

## 3. Final nuance audit — material that must remain visible
The second audit recovered or re-emphasized details that were present in the author source but easy to lose in a causal summary.

### Original Beast World
- broad biodiversity, not four heraldic animal families;
- common, rare, mythical, oceanic and plant-like forms;
- `Beast` may exceed ordinary animal taxonomy;
- the old world should feel beautiful enough that its destruction registers as extinction of a world, not destruction of a faction.

### Human host before magic
- humans already possess civilization, war, agriculture, tools, medicine and martial/breathing traditions;
- the missing piece is stable conscious Quintessence integration, not intelligence or culture;
- human cumulative culture is one contribution to the new composite, not proof of species superiority.

### Graft / Year Zero
- the graft can plausibly become the basis of a Year Zero calendar;
- early world transformation may include auroras, tremors, flora/fauna changes, new materials, metals, crystals, herbs and residual structures;
- exact spectacle remains open.

### Deep entities
- numinous/metabiological presence must not be flattened into the phrase `ecological archive`;
- aspects may be real physical bodies without being the entirety of the world-integrated entity;
- original bodies died in the old catastrophe;
- communication may begin as semantic/resonant intention and later be translated/refined by human language;
- offerings and enormous cultural authority are possible without requiring literal god-worship;
- one entity may already know the protagonist from childhood.

### Sangue / Matriz
- Sangue belongs to body, lineage and calibration, not visible purity percentages;
- Direct Concession is relational/active rather than merely higher-quality stored fluid;
- humans and Bestas both change;
- Generated Beasts can mature toward autonomous organic personhood.

### Bestas Geradas
- preserve the developmental line from Matrix/mark/germination through manifestation/materialization to autonomous person;
- maturation may be very long and variable;
- autonomous Bestas create legal, political, sexual, romantic and cultural consequences;
- the provisional **Fênix Anciã Gerada / anciã da Fênix** remains a useful example of an ancient, sacred, powerful Generated Beast, not a canonized final name/character configuration.

### Family
- mother is present;
- possible Oceanic maternal lineage remains fertile;
- powerful father and ruling uncle can be martial peers;
- parents’ political marriage may have grown into genuine love;
- old pressure for same-lineage ‘purity’ may exist without becoming the worldview endorsed by the setting;
- cousin remains legitimate heir and major world-war fighter.

### Oceanic formation
- a bounded period around fifteen-to-sixteen can involve maternal grandfather/master, instructors and another deep entity;
- that entity may possess a lighter/more humorous temperament;
- this period must remain story-active and connected to investigation/war, not a detached training vacation.

### Academy
- Coliseum, competition, rivalries, friendships, romance, humor and ordinary life matter;
- Academy also includes martial defense, medicine, alchemy, intelligence, logistics, research, artifacts and Fissure operations;
- invader dissection/anatomy can generate concrete countermeasures;
- knowledge must visibly flow from field → laboratory → medicine/war/industry → new enemy responses.

### Combat
- physical readability first: weapon, mass, stance, terrain, injury;
- speed and supernatural expression can become extreme without losing choreography;
- jumps, air control and propulsion may exist without universal free flight;
- dominance remains a provisional power concept beyond simple killing intent;
- rivals, institutions and enemies evolve alongside the protagonist.

### Fissures / other Worlds
- Fissures are discovery frontiers as well as invasion routes;
- traversal is two-way;
- other Worlds may have different laws/suppression;
- other Worlds can host long arcs;
- every important excursion should create durable consequences back home;
- enemy infiltration, collaborators and corruption-for-power are live possibilities;
- `win → consume world → bigger god` remains blocked as an automatic escalation loop, but ethical/consensual integration, liberation, salvage or other forms of transfer remain open.

### Tone and relationships
- romance, flirtation, humor, ordinary life, friendship, brutality and war are all part of the desired work;
- the protagonist may have more than one significant romantic relationship across the saga;
- strong secondary characters are not just steps on the protagonist’s ladder.

### Magic language
- resonant notation, magical geometry, runes or formal magic language may plausibly arise from nonhuman/world patterns plus cumulative human abstraction and refinement;
- no final notation system is promoted by this pass.

## 5. Historical-material policy
Old V3/V4 pages, rejected tests and superseded labs are not deleted. They must be visually demoted from the human entry route and treated as historical/archive material unless explicitly reconciled into the current encyclopedia.

The old Notion encyclopedia’s 23/08 V4 entry package is therefore historical and should no longer be presented as the current reading route.

## 6. Current creative gate after promotion
The project no longer needs another macro-premise validation loop before every development step.

1. choose one Book 1 inciting incident;
2. define one protagonist–entity relationship model with enough specificity to support that incident;
3. define one enemy champion or strategic constraint;
4. define a minimum peer/adult ensemble with independent wants;
5. stress-test those elements against ADR-0006 and Pass 50D before sequence/scene writing.
