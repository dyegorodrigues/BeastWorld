## 3. JJK upgrade
JJK is now explicitly indexed as a **whole narrative-engineering reference**, not only combat/design.

High-value lessons preserved:

### Individual power as character grammar
Shared fundamentals can coexist with intensely individualized techniques. Power can reveal how a person thinks, risks, controls space/information/body and responds under pressure.

### Rules manufacture situations
Conditions, restrictions and formal game rules are useful when they generate choice, exploitation, alliances, deadlines and conflict rather than existing only as lore.

### Gojo as strategic gravity
A strongest character matters because enemies/institutions plan around his existence. This is a major model for the future adult BeastWorld protagonist.

### Avoid the Gojo trap
BeastWorld should not depend on constantly removing its strongest protagonist so the ensemble can matter. Supporting characters require independent domains of indispensability.

### Relationships as story engines
Character sheets are insufficient. Pairs/trios/rivalries/mentorships should alter behavior, humor, ideology, weakness and decisions.

### Institutional pressure
Character breakdown or reform can emerge from personal stress interacting with structural institutional problems.

### Irreversibility with purpose
Brutality matters when consequences transform future decisions/relationships/institutions, not when death is used as a maturity sticker.

## 5. Solo Leveling / Nano Machine / Fullmetal synthesis

### Solo Leveling
Preserve:
- visible progression in movement/action;
- threshold/presence pleasure;
- anime lesson that supporting perspectives can prevent protagonist-centric monotony.

### Nano Machine
Preserve:
- pleasure of acquiring technical competence/repertoire;
- martial institution and political pressure as contexts for learning.

Do not import literal nanotech/cultivation scaffold.

### Fullmetal Alchemist
Preserve:
- few deep rules producing many personal/civilizational consequences;
- major stations can be known while characters determine connective tissue.

This becomes the strongest current antidote to BeastWorld's overengineering problem.

## 6. Complexity-budget rule
