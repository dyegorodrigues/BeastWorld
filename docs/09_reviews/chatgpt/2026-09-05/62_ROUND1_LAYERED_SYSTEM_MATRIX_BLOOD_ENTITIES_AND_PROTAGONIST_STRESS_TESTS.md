## 6. Founders — recovered historical model
The author recalls each Entity recognizing/selecting one early human Founder after graft.

Candidate sequence:
1. Entity encounters human populations in its transformed ecological region;
2. recognizes a Founder;
3. provides primordial biological material / Blood / essence;
4. actively calibrates/reorganizes a human Matrix;
5. Founder gains capacity but not downloaded technique;
6. human cumulative culture builds martial practice, medicine, alchemy, institutions and state over generations;
7. descendants inherit a stabilized/refined architecture.

This candidate naturally supports the current Working Canon phrase `first Direct Concession since the Founders`, but exact Founder ritual and title remain unresolved.

## 7. Layered power architecture
Research:
`docs/06_research/2026-09-05-round1-v3-layered-power-system-stress-tests.md`

Current strongest architecture, still hypothesis:

> **Quintessence provides the field. Blood/Matrix define living capacity. Training writes personal martial grammar. Concordance governs relationships with other living intelligences. Alchemy externalizes/formalizes processes into civilization.**

Jobs:

This prevents `resonance` from becoming a universal mystery word.

## 16. Continuity-window hypothesis
To permit genuine restoration without cheap resurrection:
- biological death need not equal instantaneous total loss of metabiological continuity;
- a bounded coherent pattern may persist temporarily under rare conditions;
- forbidden alchemy may rebuild matter around that same continuity;
- once coherence is truly lost, energy/material cannot recreate the same individual.

This remains dangerous and must face further tests before survival.
