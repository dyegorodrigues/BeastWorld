# Pass 60 — Story-Ready Skeleton and Dynamic Author Room Workflow

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Status:** `PROCESS REFRAME / STORY-READY PREPARATION / NO LORE PROMOTION / NO SCENE`

## Trigger
After Pass 59 preserved the reference-engineering atlas, the author identified a new usability problem: the project now contains good reconstructed lore, research and governance, but it is not yet obvious **what must be solved next so the actual work can begin**.

The author explicitly wants an autonomous, research-capable, multi-option creative process that connects magic/martial systems, Dynasties/Houses, characters, relationships, Academy, plot, progression, time skip and saga structure without recreating the prior complexity spiral.

## Diagnosis
The existing four P0 gates remain valid as **scene authorization gates**, but they are too close to the chapter level to serve as the author's mental model for the whole remaining development.

The missing layer is a **Story-Ready Skeleton**: the minimum interoperable set of world, character and plot decisions required to make intelligent story choices.

## Decision — process only
Created:

`docs/00_governance/STORY_READY_AUTHOR_ROOM_WORKFLOW.md`

Human-facing development is reframed around four questions:

1. How does the world work?
2. Who lives in it and what do they want?
3. What happens first that cannot be taken back?
4. How does that first story grow into the larger saga?

Governance remains backstage.

## Recommended round order

1. **Power & combat skeleton** — 3 candidate generative grammars.
2. **Society & naming architecture** — define institutional meanings before names.
3. **Protagonist + relationship web** — minimum ensemble and competence ecology.
4. **Book 1 architectures** — 3 complete causal spines, not isolated scenes.
5. **Select/fuse** — close the four existing P0 scene gates.
6. **Youth → adult bridge** — compare time-skip/Director models only after Book 1 exposes what would be skipped.
7. **Sequence map** — chapters/subplots/reveals/quiet life/action.
8. **Scene creation** — use Creative Scene Gate.

This order is braided rather than rigid: story questions may expose missing mechanics, and mechanics should be solved only far enough to support character choice and story consequence.

## Round protocol
For each major question:

- load only relevant current context and reference research;
- generate normally 3 genuinely different models;
- show felt image + construction logic + consequences + risks + resemblance hazards;
- allow author rejection/hybridization in plain language;
- stress-test preferred model;
- promote only explicitly accepted pieces;
- preserve rejected alternatives and reasons;
- update Notion/GitHub at meaningful milestones, not every conversational sentence.

## Research reinforcement
Fresh verification during this pass reinforced three useful design lessons:

- official *Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood* material demonstrates how a small alchemical rule set and one taboo rapidly expand from two brothers' personal loss into military, national, ethnic and conspiratorial consequence;
- official *Jujutsu Kaisen* Shibuya interviews explicitly describe Gojo's sealing as the condition that lets multiple characters, curses and agendas explode into a broader ensemble conflict;
- *Solo Leveling* producers describe progression as something visible in the protagonist's changing action, while also noting that adaptation needed more psychological depth from other characters to avoid monotony from an overly protagonist-centered structure.

These remain reference lessons, not BeastWorld lore.

## No lore promotion
Pass 60 does not decide:

- final magic/martial mechanics;
- Dynasty/House nomenclature;
- Book 1 incident or climax;
- ensemble roster;
- time-skip size;
- Director age/status;
- final titles/names.

The next creative work is **Round 1: Power & Combat Skeleton**, while keeping Book 1 and character consequences visible during the comparison.
