## 3. Communication architecture
Created:

`docs/00_governance/AUTHOR_EXPLANATION_STYLE_AND_TECHNICAL_LEXICON_2026-09-05.md`

The target author-facing explanation now has three cooperating layers:

1. **felt image** — make the idea visible and emotionally/intuitively graspable;
2. **construction logic** — cause, mechanism, vocabulary, constraint, consequence and status;
3. **backstage note** — compact research/reference lens and its boundary when useful.

This is a composition principle, not a rigid answer template.

## 4. Anti-failure rules
Blocked explanation failures:

- dry technical specification that destroys the imagined world;
- empty hype/superlatives that never identify why an idea works;
- jargon introduced without translation;
- poetic language that obscures status or mechanism;
- references transplanted because they are admired rather than studied for a function;
- scientific analogy presented as literal proof of fictional biology;
- useful technical vocabulary deleted in the name of accessibility;
- technical vocabulary forced into reader-facing prose merely because it exists backstage.
