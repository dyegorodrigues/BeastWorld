## Alternative architectures

When the problem is structural, provide 2–4 viable alternatives rather than forcing a single answer prematurely.

For each:

- name;
- mechanism;
- strengths;
- weaknesses;
- assumptions;
- failure modes;
- originality/copy risk;
- production/narrative cost;
- reversibility.

- If this is true, what obvious exploit exists?
- Who would monopolize it?
- What black market appears?

- What accidental contradiction does it create elsewhere?
