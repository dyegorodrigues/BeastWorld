# Author source record — reference atlas, JJK/Gojo, Fog Hill and full-story engineering

## Authority
This record captures the author's follow-up after the Fog Hill comparative-research round and Pass 58 communication-fidelity work.

## Author directive
The author explicitly requires that useful research, analysis, inspirations and derived creative insights not remain trapped only in chat memory. They must be preserved in GitHub/Notion in a form that future conversations can recover without asking him to restate the references or mental model.

The intended reference study is **not limited to fight choreography**. For each major inspiration, research the work as a complete narrative/production system where useful, including:

- world premise and cosmology;
- magic/power mechanics and how rules generate situations;
- character construction and distinctiveness;
- relationships, teams, rivalries, mentors and antagonist dynamics;
- institutional structure;
- plot architecture and progression;
- challenge/escalation design;
- memorable scene/moment construction;
- combat grammar, weapons, martial arts and audiovisual staging;
- character growth and how new competencies become visible;
- ensemble relevance when one character becomes extremely powerful;
- what should be studied, what should stay quarantined, and how BeastWorld can transform rather than merely transplant the reference.

## Jujutsu Kaisen emphasis
Jujutsu Kaisen is a major inspiration and had been underrepresented in the earlier reference scaffold.

The author's interest includes, but is not limited to:

- Gojo Satoru as a major inspiration for the BeastWorld protagonist's presence, personality/power fantasy and later adult/Director potential;
- the larger cursed-energy system, including layered techniques, reversals/advanced applications, domains, vows/conditions and individual signatures;
- techniques/powers as character identity rather than a universal list of identical spells;
- character relationships and group dynamics;
- villains and nonhuman opponents with their own values/claims rather than generic evil;
- brutal irreversible turning points;
- how a very powerful character changes the strategy, politics and threat design of the whole world;
- how ensemble characters remain meaningful despite the existence of an exceptional strongest figure;
- how scenes, character moments and conflicts are generated by the interaction of people, rules and institutions, not only by power escalation.

The author does **not** want BeastWorld to become Jujutsu Kaisen with renamed concepts.

## Adult Director / chronology status
The author restated that the protagonist has a meaningful adolescent growth phase, learning the world and developing through the first major story period.

A later time skip to a substantially older, extremely capable protagonist who may become Director remains a live possibility. The author floated possibilities such as:

- a time skip near the end of Book 1;
- a time skip between Book 1 and Book 2;
- another structure if it produces a better saga.

This is **not a selected chronology** and must remain an architecture question rather than being silently promoted.

The adult/Director phase is expected to carry a stronger, more brutal, high-pressure tone while still preserving the prior character and world development.

## Ensemble requirement
A future strongest/Director protagonist must not make everyone else useless.

The author explicitly wants a strong cast with meaningful character construction, relationships, moments and independent relevance. The solution should not be merely to make every opponent stronger numerically or to remove/seal/kill the protagonist whenever other characters need to act.

Research should therefore examine forms of indispensability beyond raw duel strength: specialized combat, simultaneous fronts, intelligence, medicine, science, logistics, politics, command, negotiation, infiltration, relationships, Beast expertise, Other-World knowledge and other domains that emerge naturally from BeastWorld.

## Fog Hill / Solo Leveling / Nano Machine / Fullmetal and wider atlas
The Fog Hill round was valuable not just for visual style but for the complete relationship among combat, character, weapons, world and dramatic function.

The author wants the same broad treatment for the major inspiration set, especially:

- Fog Hill of Five Elements;
- Jujutsu Kaisen;
- Solo Leveling;
- Nano Machine;
- Fullmetal Alchemist;
- and other already indexed references such as Hunter × Hunter, World Trigger, TBATE, One-Punch Man, Kaiju No. 8 and relevant anime/manhua/donghua/webcomic works.

The set is not exhaustive. Future research may add a reference when it answers a concrete design question.

## Visual direction
The author is highly inspired by Fog Hill's kinetic martial-combat intelligence but does not want BeastWorld visually locked to Chinese ink-wash/xianxia aesthetics.

Current visual preference remains closer to the clean, dark, cinematic character-design language associated with Solo Leveling, Nano Machine and other modern anime/manhwa references, while potentially extracting Fog Hill's body/weapon/terrain intelligence and constructing a distinct BeastWorld audiovisual signature for Quintessence and bestial power.

This is visual-development direction, not final art canon.

## Complexity concern
The author again expressed concern that AI-assisted development can turn a naturally flowing idea into excessive technical complexity and endless problem solving.

Research should help simplify construction, not merely create more documents. A useful rule/mechanism should justify itself through character, story, combat, institution, discovery, consequence or later decision value.

## Preservation requirement
The Fog Hill/JJK/reference-engineering research and strong derived hypotheses must be captured in a stable research atlas and routed from the Second Brain. Chat responses alone are not sufficient long-term memory.

## No lore promotion
This source authorizes research capture, synthesis and story-design hypotheses. It does **not** promote:

- a JJK-like curse system;
- progression-by-verbs as final mechanics;
- biomechanical combat categories;
- three choreography states;
- a specific time skip;
- a fixed Director chronology;
- any copied Domain/Binding-Vow/Reverse-Technique analogue;
- any character death or plot event;
- any reference-derived mechanic.

ADR-0006 remains the current lore-promotion boundary.
