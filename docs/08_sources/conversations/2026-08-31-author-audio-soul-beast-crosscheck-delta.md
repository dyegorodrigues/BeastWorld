## Recovered details that materially change interpretation

### 1. `Animalidade`
The control transcript recovered an entire concept absent from the first pass:

> When the Beast is reintegrated/fused into the human body, the result is **animalidade** — a state of more complete fusion between human, bestial magic, the magical world/Quintessence and the “pure” bestial energy.

This means the Beast is not only an external summon. It has at least one internal mode in which its existence changes body, senses, instincts and martial expression.

### 2. Perfect fusion / internal state
The author explicitly described “fusão perfeita” while the Beast is reintegrated in the person. This suggests a tactical continuum:

- Beast internal/reintegrated;
- partial expression;
- external materialization;
- possibly a high-risk full overlap/fusion.

No terminology or final mechanics are approved.

### 3. Evolution concern and explicit balancing doubt
After imagining salamander/snake → wyvern/wyrm → dragon/mythic Beast, the author explicitly stated:

> “Eu não sei como equilibrar isso.”

Therefore linear species evolution is a brainstorm problem, not an endorsed rule.

### 4. Avoiding Pokémon is an explicit design constraint
The author’s intent is not merely cosmetic. The system must avoid:

- collection;
- trainer/pet hierarchy;
- interchangeable monsters;
- species ladder as a universal level system;
- creatures without true agency.

The Beast must remain conscious, autonomous, emotionally real and capable of existing independently.

### 5. Natural/organic Beasts versus human-generated/inherited Beasts
The control transcript reinforces a distinction between:

- naturally born/organic magical creatures;
- Beasts generated through a human anchoring process;
- inherited/persistent Beasts whose previous human died.

Whether all possess a nucleus or only persistent/inherited forms do remains open.

### 6. Early-life rite as brainstorm, not decision
The author considered that children may receive a ritual from a young age and begin creating the Beast. This is still an open possibility, not a canonized universal ceremony.

### 7. Primordials as the “system”
The author proposed that Primordial manifestations themselves might function, “entre aspas”, as **the system** for one or a few characters. This does not imply UI, quests, numerical stats or videogame logic. It suggests a living intelligence that grants access, interprets, teaches, withholds or reorganizes the character’s interface.

### 8. Protagonist — source uncertainty remains important
The author repeatedly corrected himself about:

- whether the protagonist is an heir;
- who his father is in the dynastic hierarchy;
- whether the ancient nucleus is found, shown or deliberately granted;
- whether the Primordial gives the same original Essence/Blood as to the founder;
- whether the core belongs to the first elder, guardian, sovereign or chosen founder.

All these are intentionally unresolved.

### 9. Tomb wording conflict
The two transcript passes conflict on whether the author said:

- “eu imaginei ele entrando numa tumba”; or
- “eu **não** imaginei ele entrando numa tumba”.

Do not resolve this by inference. The underlying design question is preserved: should the ancestral nucleus be discovered as treasure, granted by the Primordial, inherited institutionally, or germinated anew?

### 10. Character combat psychology
The control transcript confirms:

- social mode: loyal/cool, funny, petulant, mocking;
- initial combat mode: reserved, cold, quiet;
- when pushed: begins to enjoy power, violence, resistance and even injury;
- explicit rejection of becoming a simple Kenpachi Zaraki copy.

This is character exploration, not finalized personality.
