2. The present world is a genuine new composite, not a simple restoration of the old Beast World and not merely a human world with magic added.
3. Human and Beast development is reciprocal and cumulative across biology, culture, institutions and ecology.
4. The civilization responsible for the old near-extinction eventually finds the refuge/current BeastWorld again.
5. The protagonist’s main story begins around age fifteen; the adult Director remains a future state that must be earned through the saga.
6. The Direct Concession during the first coordinated Fissure campaign remains the current Book 1 axis.
7. Reciprocal development versus forced/extractive incorporation remains the current thematic opposition, without requiring a simplistic moral taxonomy for whole peoples.
8. The cousin remains the legitimate designated successor and an independently formidable combatant; succession is not the whole premise.
