## New or sharpened deltas

### Regeneration
- magical Beasts and sufficiently evolved humans may regenerate skin, vessels, bone, organs, peripheral structures and, at rare high levels, limbs;
- organ regeneration is allowed; immediate loss of function must still be stabilized;
- capacity depends on development, training, control, Matrix/source quality, granted blood quality, Resonance, species/lineage aptitude, retained pattern, matter, Quintessence and medical support;
- high combat power does not automatically equal equal healing mastery;
- cursed, anti-resonant or pattern-breaking injuries can slow, freeze or deform regeneration;
- medical alchemy, field stabilization, surgery, regenerative beds/chambers, rare compounds and rehabilitation remain important;
- Jujutsu Kaisen reverse cursed technique is a comparative inspiration only, not the BeastWorld mechanism.

### Natural Beasts and a possible mythic state
- the author proposes testing a state above ordinary ancient development for extremely rare Natural Beasts, provisionally called mythic;
- this may include biological humanoid metamorphosis without making age, morphology or the word “mythic” a universal power rank;
- “Ancient/Elder” and house offices remain naming problems rather than closed terminology.

### Phoenix
- she is an autonomous Generated Beast from an important older generation and may be among the House’s strongest/highest-potential figures;
- she need not be Sovereign or formal leader;
- a strong function is protection, education and maturation of younger humans/Generated Beasts plus ecological balance;
- public demeanor is serious, controlled and dangerous; selective cracks around the 35-year-old Director reveal genuine mutual sexual attraction;
- the Director is dominant, provocative and self-controlled, not shy, submissive or reduced to a cheesy confession;
- no pornography and no transformation staged merely to test attraction.

### Combat and action language
- combat must combine weapons, martial bodywork and magic;
- desired modes include duel, one-versus-many, battlefield insertion, sword exchanges, grappling, elbows, kicks, brutal close quarters and large but legible magical effects;
- references include Solo Leveling, Jujutsu Kaisen, Naruto/Madara, Bleach, Nano Machine, I’m an Evil God and other anime/manhwa/martial fantasy, used selectively rather than copied;
- the author is looking for the correct vocabulary for animation/choreography, speed, impact, transitions, key moments and biomechanics.

### Director battlefield proof
- possible sequence: aerial arrival with Dragon/partner; controlled missile-like drop; near-teleport acceleration; directed release of stored kinetic/magical force into a command corridor; lateral sword traversal; voluntary shift to barehanded close combat after planting or abandoning the sword;
- violence should reveal strategic intelligence, predatory exhilaration and personality rather than treating soldiers as consequence-free visual confetti.

### Visual design and hair
- visual exploration combines epic Western/House-of-the-Dragon weight with neo-ronin movement logic, Greco-Roman/late-antique civic-military silhouette and European neo-medieval construction;
- this remains a design thesis, not a finalized costume style or copied historical culture;
- the Director may alternate long hair and military-short hair; accelerated regrowth follows bestial regeneration and can mark time, missions and identity;
- limited somatic control is possible, but instant arbitrary hairstyle generation should not erase the meaning of cutting/growing hair;
- the author may provide generated reference images later.
