## Regeneration — explicit author direction

The author strongly prefers meaningful regenerative capacity for humans transformed by the ritual/Matrix/Mark process and for evolved Beasts. Regeneration should be graded rather than binary:

- skin and superficial tissue;
- blood vessels;
- muscle;
- bone;
- nerves;
- organs;
- small peripheral structures/digits;
- entire limbs and major structures at exceptional levels.

The ceiling and speed may depend on:

- quality/potency of the donated bestial material;
- donor Beast development/ancestry;
- compatibility and inherited/genetic potential;
- success of the ritual and integration;
- training and depth of Resonance;
- personal development;
- current reserves, nourishment, Quintessence and recovery conditions;
- medical/alchemical assistance.

Minor or moderate damage may repair automatically and visibly. Large structural losses require withdrawal from combat, protection, rest, meditation/absorption, food/biomass, special resources and possibly master healers/alchemists.

The author accepts that curses, hostile abilities, anti-regenerative damage or pattern disruption may slow, freeze or corrupt healing and create a necessary role for advanced medical intervention.

Brain injury is not automatically fatal for highly developed transformed humans. A puncture, cut or partial loss may be repaired if enough central continuity and regenerative control remain. Complete destruction of the brain/central continuity without an exceptional preserved Core remains a plausible true-death boundary.
