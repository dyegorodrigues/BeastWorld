## Important distinction

The earlier wording treated “being ready,” “becoming Mythic,” “acquiring humanoid capability” and “choosing a current form” as if they were one decision. The author correction separates them:

1. a Natural Beast develops the prerequisites;
2. the Mythic passage actually occurs;
3. its biology now includes reversible humanoid modulation;
4. the person chooses when or whether to use it.

Preparedness is not Mythic status. Humanoid use is not the Mythic passage itself.
