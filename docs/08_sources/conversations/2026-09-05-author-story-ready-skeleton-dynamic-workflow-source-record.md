# Author source record — story-ready skeleton and dynamic creative workflow

## Author intent
The author wants to move BeastWorld from reconstructed/aligned worldbuilding into an actually creatable work without returning to uncontrolled technical complexity.

The author explicitly asks for an autonomous, research-capable creative-room process that can:

- identify what still needs to be defined before the work can truly begin;
- resolve the magical/martial system enough for characters and scenes to function;
- resolve the meaning and nomenclature of Dynasties/Houses/related institutions;
- create characters, relationships, narrative situations and long-term evolution rather than only combat systems;
- generate multiple strong alternatives rather than forcing one answer;
- research relevant books, anime, manga/manhwa/donghua, webcomics, series and films at any layer of construction;
- integrate system, character, ensemble, institution, plot and audiovisual action rather than developing them as isolated departments;
- explore the protagonist's youth-to-adult/Director transition without prematurely locking the time-skip structure;
- keep the story from becoming nonstop combat or a shallow power fantasy;
- continue saving decisions, rejected alternatives, research rationale, open questions and current state into Notion/GitHub so good ideas do not die in chat memory;
- keep the human-facing process simple enough that the author can see what is happening without having to operate the governance machinery.

## Important nuance
The author does not want to be constrained by the exact wording of his request. The assistant is explicitly authorized to think beyond the prompt, identify better questions, research proactively and propose alternative architectures.

The author is unsure whether the youth phase should occupy one book, whether Book 2 should open after a large time skip, or whether another structure would work better. This remains OPEN.

The author's intuition remains that the adult phase, especially if/when the protagonist becomes Director and exceptionally powerful, is where the story can become more frenetic, tense and civilizational in scale. The youth phase must therefore earn that future rather than function as disposable tutorial material.

## Process requirement
The user-facing workflow should be a creative room, not a governance dashboard.

Governance should continue backstage:

- source captured;
- alternatives/status preserved;
- explicit promotion only after author acceptance;
- rejected/superseded material archived rather than silently deleted;
- periodic regression checks;
- Notion/GitHub kept synchronized at meaningful milestones.

## No lore promotion
This source authorizes a workflow/process change and new research/development rounds. It does not itself decide the final magic system, Dynasty nomenclature, Book 1 structure, ensemble, time skip or Director age.
