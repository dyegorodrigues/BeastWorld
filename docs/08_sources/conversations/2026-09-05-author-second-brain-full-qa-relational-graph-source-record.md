# Author source record — full QA of the rebuilt Second Brain

## Authority
This record captures the author’s explicit follow-up after the Pass 56 Notion/GitHub rebuild.

## Author directive
The author reviewed the new Notion at a high level, considered it substantially improved, and requested a second complete verification rather than immediate continuation of story design.

The requested standard is not merely technical consistency. The Second Brain must:

- preserve the richness of the author’s ideas, explanations, visualizations and insights;
- remain detailed enough that the author can later reread it and recover what he meant, not only the final conclusion;
- explain what each concept is, how it works, why it exists and what it connects to;
- make current state, hypothesis, open question, superseded material and source provenance unmistakable;
- use nodes, tags, relations and other Notion best practices to make the knowledge graph genuinely navigable;
- keep the human-readable encyclopedia and the structured relational layer connected rather than operating as two independent systems;
- verify GitHub after Notion and repair any stale route, pointer or governance text that could reintroduce superseded material;
- favor quality and semantic completeness over speed.

## Interpretation
This author source authorizes a **non-destructive relational reactivation and editorial QA pass** over the Pass 56 Second Brain.

It does not authorize promotion of unresolved lore. Any new structural knowledge-management decision may be recorded as an ADR, but lore status must remain governed by ADR-0006 and `CANON_POLICY.md`.

## QA acceptance criteria
The pass should only close after verifying:

1. every current encyclopedia module is readable and sufficiently explanatory;
2. cross-module dependencies and downstream consequences are visible;
3. current concepts are represented in the relational registry rather than leaving the graph frozen in V3/V4;
4. tags are retrieval aids, not substitutes for epistemic status;
5. current decisions and sources are represented in Decision Log and Source Library;
6. open questions remain queryable through the research pipeline;
7. superseded/rejected material remains preserved but visually and relationally demoted;
8. GitHub HOT routing cannot still claim that Pass 55 is awaiting author validation;
9. Notion and GitHub identify the same current route and next gate;
10. no source or historical evidence is deleted merely to make the interface look clean.
