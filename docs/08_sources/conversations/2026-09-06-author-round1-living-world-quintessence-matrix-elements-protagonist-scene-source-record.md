## 2. Living World clarification
The author's term `Mundo Vivo` does **not** mean merely `planet with a biosphere`.

Intended image:
- an ordinary inhabited planet can contain abundant life without being a Living World in the BeastWorld technical sense;
- a Living World has crossed a Quintessential/metabiological threshold in which planetary matter, biosphere, Quintessence and large-scale organization become a **planetary living subject/system with distributed agency**;
- it can preserve itself, react, adapt, reorganize, learn and pursue continued evolution without needing a human-like inner monologue;
- the author repeatedly describes it as `conscious but not conscious`, `an abstract divinity but not a god`, and something that `wants to evolve/save itself`; these are attempts to visualize planetary-scale agency, not claims of a human mind inside a globe;
- the pre-graft human host can be biologically rich yet still not be a fully awakened Living World in this sense.

Useful writer-room concepts to test, not final in-world names: `agência planetária metabiológica`, `consciência distribuída/policêntrica`, cautious `teleonomia planetária`, `ontologia metabiológica`, `numinoso`.

## 3. Deep Entities clarified: one-and-many planetary persons
The author corrected the phrase `organs that acquired personhood` if it implies unrelated organs later becoming people.

Stronger intended image:
- the old Living World itself generates/segments/focalizes conscious metabiological Entities from its own Quintessential planetary life;
- they are simultaneously **the world and themselves**: one system with individuated centers of memory, temperament, intelligence and agency;
- they are fully integrated with the Living World, not merely deeply connected external creatures;
- on the original world they possessed real ancient biological bodies and greater autonomy/mobility while remaining continuous with the planet;
- they learn, evolve, remember, prefer, disagree and can form relationships;
- civilizationally they may be treated as near-divine or sacred, but are neither omnipotent external gods nor generic nature spirits;
- current material manifestations can be physically real, touch and carry weight, yet represent only a bounded localized aspect of the whole Entity;
- ordinary blood may not be the right image for current aspects because those bodies may be condensed from Quintessence + planetary matter/life;
- destruction of the sustaining Living World/world-core/ecological organization would eventually kill the Entities too;
- the original catastrophe/sacrifice may have destroyed their more autonomous original biological bodies and forced a deeper/permanent integration into the surviving remnant.

### Bestial origins remain open but important
The author currently imagines the Entities as primordial biological matrices/origins around which broad families of Beast life emerged and diversified. Do **not** reduce the whole biosphere to four mascot species. The exact degree of literal ancestry versus ecological/pattern architecture remains to be stress-tested.

The author still strongly visualizes **four** old-world Entities, but the number is not declared a sacred universal law; better architecture can still be tested.

## 4. Self-capability versus relational perception
Do not confuse:
- protagonist's own enhanced senses, reflexes, threat detection, instinct, bestial perception and trained reading; with
- information supplied by another living subject through a Beast/Entity relationship.

Example: detecting danger because his own enhanced senses notice it is self-capability. Receiving a perception/impression because the Beast noticed something and chose to share it is relational. Shared vision, impressions, semantic contact or mental imagery can be investigated without creating a game `System`.

## 6. Strong Quintessence candidate to test — one substrate, three regimes
`HYPOTHESIS / NOT CANON`

Instead of copying a Mana + Aether dual system:
1. **free/cosmic Quintessence** — universal substrate present through the cosmos in varying concentration;
2. **world-organized Quintessence** — a Living World concentrates/modulates/cycles that substrate into a coherent local signature;
3. **assimilated/bound Quintessence** — organisms, materials and Matrix bind/metabolize/store structured Quintessence internally.

Potential consequences:
- permanent metabiological/bodily adaptations persist even in poor ambient conditions;
- active reinforcement can spend assimilated/internal reserves;
- large projection/environmental work depends more on ambient/world-organized Quintessence;
- foreign Worlds alter recharge, efficiency and compatibility rather than simply toggling magic off;
- crystals, organs, materials or artifacts may carry structured reserves;
- the pre-graft human host can be rich in raw/pure Quintessence potential without yet possessing planetary-scale Living-World organization.

This elegantly matches the author's old `pure/virgin star` image but is not promoted yet.

## 7. Matrix clarification — ritual geometry is not the internal Matrix
The author still strongly imagines the Founding integration as **alchemy/transmutation with geometry/symbolic structure**.

Candidate distinction:
- **external integration geometry / ritual architecture:** a trained procedure that uses matter, Blood/Essence, Quintessence, body, timing, materials and possibly Entity calibration to induce/recalibrate a transformation;
- **internal living Matrix:** the distributed biological/metabiological organization that results and then develops/remodels with the organism.

This keeps the alchemical image without placing a literal Fullmetal-style transmutation circle inside every body.

### Matrix development/regeneration questions
The author likes the distributed Matrix + possible marrow/axial anchoring hypothesis and asks whether it grows/evolves with the person. Strong direction to test:
- Matrix matures/remodels through growth, training, injury, treatment and Concession;
- distributed organization permits rerouting around local damage;
- limb loss need not permanently cripple advanced characters;
- regeneration can reconstruct severe loss using the body's living pattern plus time, matter, Quintessence, medicine/alchemy/catalysts;
- costs/rates vary and death remains real.

`Losing coherence` should never be unexplained jargon. Plain meaning: living tissues stop coordinating the same integrated bestial/Quintessential pattern correctly under load, causing reinforcement, healing, perception, timing or energy routing to fall out of sync. This can be temporary/treatable rather than automatic permanent disability.

## 9. Elemental surface without Avatar/Five-Element prison
The author wants **recognizable elemental magic** while refusing a simplistic `Fire Nation / Dragon House = fire spells` architecture.

Desired structure:
- Dinasties/states remain culturally, biologically and bestially diverse;
- each may have a deep signature/fundamental mode associated with its Entity/history;
- visible elements are important surfaces of that deeper mode, not the total definition;
- mastery can generate derived expressions;
- long regional evolution may make some derivatives naturally common in subpopulations/Beast lineages.

Specific examples the author wants to retain as possibilities:
- Dragon/paternal line: Fire is primary/visible; **Lightning** is a desired high-mastery/derived expression for the protagonist;
- Oceanic: Water primary/visible; Ice can be a derivative or regionally stabilized variation;
- Air/Fênix: Wind/Air primary; Sound/vibration is a possible derivative;
- Earth/Qilin: Earth/structure/mineral/nature family remains to be refined; plant/growth should not be assumed automatically.

Avatar is useful for body-magic integration and elemental derivatives, but BeastWorld should feel more violent, weapon-based, adaptive and physically savage; Fog Hill is closer to desired combat intensity.
