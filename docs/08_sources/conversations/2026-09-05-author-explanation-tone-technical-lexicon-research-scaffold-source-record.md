# Author source record — explanation tone, technical vocabulary and research scaffold

## Authority
This record captures the author's final QA request after Pass 57.

## Author problem statement
The project has repeatedly suffered from a presentation failure: a strong idea can feel weak, generic or embarrassing when explained in dry technical prose, while the opposite failure is also unacceptable — decorative, enthusiastic prose that repeatedly calls ideas wonderful without explaining why they work.

The author wants BeastWorld development to preserve **both the felt image and the construction logic**.

## Required author-facing explanation quality
When discussing worldbuilding, narrative architecture, magic, biology, entities, institutions or story mechanics with the author:

- write with enough literary clarity that the idea can be visualized and emotionally understood;
- do not drown the explanation in sterile engineering language;
- do not compensate for a weak idea with empty praise, hype or excessive superlatives;
- preserve precise construction vocabulary when it carries a useful distinction;
- explain technical terms in normal language on first use;
- distinguish the beautiful/reader-facing image from backstage mechanism, hypothesis and evidence;
- say why something works, what problem it solves, what risk it introduces and what remains unresolved;
- when useful, give a compact backstage note showing the scientific, biological, historical, narrative or audiovisual reference being used and its limit;
- references are for **study and engineering**, not automatic transplantation into BeastWorld;
- do not make the author read a research paper in order to feel the idea, and do not hide all research merely because the final novel should not expose it.

## Recovered terminology that must not be lost
The author's source explicitly recovers the phrase **`ontologia metabiológica`** while describing the deep entities/Primordials.

The associated mental model is that they are not ordinary spirits piloting flesh and not external gods standing outside nature. They are imagined as **living, conscious functions of a Living World**, materially continuous enough with ecology, Quintessence and world-processes that they may, under limits, **metabolize the world and form real material bodies/aspects**.

This is a writer-room/construction term unless later promoted as an in-world scholarly expression.

Related useful backstage vocabulary includes:

- `Mundo Vivo` / Living World;
- `núcleo do mundo` / world-core — placeholder for deep coupling of matter, Quintessence and life;
- `função do mundo` / world-function — differentiated governing intelligence within the whole;
- `regulador ecológico` / `arquivo ecológico` — partial analytical lenses, never the entity's entire identity;
- `aspecto material` / embodiment — a real bounded body/expression, not a hologram and not the total being;
- `carga funcional`, `throughput`, `capacidade de manifestação`, distance/duration constraints — backstage ways of asking what a world-integrated being can safely externalize;
- `simbiogênese` — analogy for novelty emerging through durable biological integration;
- `holobionte` — cautious analogy for a composite living system, never proof that all parts form one harmonious evolutionary individual;
- `construção de nicho` / ecological inheritance — organisms/cultures changing environments that then reshape later development;
- `cultura cumulativa` — human ability to accumulate, formalize and transmit adaptive knowledge;
- `coevolução` / Red-Queen-type reciprocal adaptation — useful for an enemy that learns back rather than a static escalation ladder;
- `continuidade adaptativa` — preferable to a metaphysical destiny of endless upward evolution;
- `integração recíproca` versus `incorporação forçada/extrativa` — current structural contrast;
- `calibração`, `coerência`, `memória ecológica`, `padrão de linhagem` — useful construction terms where mechanisms genuinely depend on them.

None of these technical labels becomes final reader-facing or in-world vocabulary merely by being useful backstage.

## Research and reference rule
The author wants the research base preserved because it helps BeastWorld feel biologically, civilizationally and narratively coherent.

Relevant research lenses already gathered in Pass 55 include:

- symbiogenesis/endosymbiosis;
- niche construction and ecological inheritance;
- human cumulative culture;
- host–microbiome / holobiont literature, with explicit critical caveats;
- reciprocal coevolution / Red Queen dynamics;
- planetary-scale collective intelligence as analogy, not proof of a conscious planet.

Fiction/audiovisual references already used as comparative engineering include, among others, World Trigger, Kaiju No. 8, The Beginning After the End, One-Punch Man, Solo Leveling, Nano Machine, Fog Hill of Five Elements, Hunter × Hunter, Dragon Ball's Beerus horizon expansion, Final Fantasy VII, Nausicaä, Princess Mononoke and Xenoblade Chronicles 3.

For every reference, preserve the distinction:

**what to study → why it helps → what must not be copied → how BeastWorld's mechanism/experience remains different.**

## Cross-conversation requirement
A new conversation should not recover only lore facts. It must also recover:

1. the author's preferred explanation balance;
2. important writer-room terminology;
3. the research/reference scaffold supporting major construction choices;
4. the distinction between backstage language and in-world language;
5. later audits that recovered details missed by earlier summaries.

Examples named in this record are minimum checks, not an exhaustive boundary. Future rehydration must follow the current source hierarchy and load additional relevant nuances rather than treating this one message as the totality of the author's intent.

## No lore promotion
This source changes documentation and communication fidelity. It does not promote unresolved entity mechanics, scientific analogies or research vocabulary into canon.
