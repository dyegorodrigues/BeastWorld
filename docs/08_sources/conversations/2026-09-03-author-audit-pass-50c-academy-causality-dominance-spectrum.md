## 1. Generated Beast

The settled developmental model remains correct:

1. a human receives/develops Matrix and Mark;
2. Germination begins;
3. a new bestial person develops inside the human environment;
4. the person learns, speaks, perceives and manifests external field bodies;
5. its Core/continuity remains protected in the human during this phase;
6. at completion it constructs a definitive biological organism;
7. continuity transfers to that organism;

## 4. Dominance

Working reader-facing term remains **Dominance**. “Precedence” is retired. “Primordial” may describe origin only if needed.

Dominance may produce a spectrum:

- attention capture;
- fear/caution;
- hesitation or loss of focus;
- involuntary defensive freezing;
- submission posture or collapse;
- at overwhelming levels, complete suppression of action, destruction of an unstable manifestation, physiological collapse or possible death.

It cannot manufacture love, desire, loyalty, belief or ownership. Extreme bodily suppression is possible; mind/personality rewriting is not implied.

## 7. Durable authorial lesson

Before drafting a scene:

1. name every actor and object;
2. state the normal activity;
3. identify the exact trigger;
4. show the exact physical danger;
5. assign one visible action to each responder;
6. show why each action is necessary;
7. distinguish consequence from automatic blame;
8. only then write dialogue or visual poetry.
