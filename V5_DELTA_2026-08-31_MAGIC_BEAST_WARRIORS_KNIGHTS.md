## Leader synthesis — still NOT-CANON

The strongest current model is a **reciprocal evolutionary symbiosis** rather than a pet/summon system:

- Beasts: high native Quintessence coherence.
- Humans: high developmental/cultural/martial plasticity.
- Bestial Matrix + Mark + Living World can produce a third autonomous life.
- Not every marked human germinates an autonomous Beast Soul; non-germinated martial mages remain viable and potentially elite.
- Generated Beast develops on several independent axes: coherence/density, autonomy, integration, range/persistence and principle mastery.
- External Beast can fight independently, but shares a coupled metabolic/resonant ecology with the host; it is not a free second 100% battery.
- `Animalidade` remains useful as the integrated internal state; full spectral armor is better modeled as a **Convergence War-Body**, not animal morphing.
- Mature generated Beasts may survive host death as autonomous persons and learn humanoid compression/social/kinetic forms without human shape becoming “superior evolution.”
- Fôlego remains the best user-facing fatigue concept; backstage limits are metabolic/structural + neural/cognitive + resonant coherence.
- Houses can inherit Mark/Echo compatibility and stratify access to matrices without owning the absolute power ceiling.
- Direct living voluntary Beast material differs from harvested/stored material through active coherence/intent, not a simple purity percentage.
- Primordials may have initiated the ritual because the cycle lets magical life acquire human-like plasticity/cultural learning while humans gain stable magical access; the Living World receives adaptive defenders and new biodiversity.
- Corruption should have local human routes before foreign-world influence appears.
- Director’s advantages should be one causal chain: mixed-pattern failure → martial mastery → Primordial reconfiguration → ancestral remnant as developmental scaffold → new draconic organism, not stacked chosen-one gifts.
